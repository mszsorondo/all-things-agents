# IDEAL Process Model

## Process Overview and Purpose
The IDEAL process model for patient treatment streamlines the patient journey from initial registration through examinations, diagnosis, treatment, and eventual discharge. The aim is to enhance operational efficiency, reduce wait times, and improve patient outcomes by minimizing unnecessary rework and ensuring a seamless healthcare experience. This optimized process reflects best practices in governance and patient care.

## Key Stakeholders and Roles Involved
- **Patients**: Individuals seeking medical attention, ensuring their experience is efficient and satisfactory.
- **Healthcare Practitioners**: Includes general practitioners, specialists, and nurses focused on delivering timely and effective care.
- **Administrative Staff**: Streamlined registration and support staff managing patient flow and record keeping.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Process Flow

### Start Conditions and Triggers
The process commences with the registration of patients who can either:
1. **Register at FD** (family doctor) - undergoing routine care.
2. **Register at ER** (emergency room) - for urgent medical needs.

Both registration points direct the patient to an immediate examination phase.

### Process Activities/Tasks in Optimized Sequence
1. **Register at FD or ER**: Patient registration is efficiently processed, ensuring quick access to services.
   - Immediately directs to:
2. **Examination**: Conducted right after registration without unnecessary waiting time.
3. **Diagnosis**: The practitioner evaluates the patient's condition based on examination results.
   - Leads directly to:
4. **Treatment**: The relevant treatment plan is executed without superfluous steps.
   - Outcomes:
     - If **Treatment Successful**, proceed to:
       - **Discharge**: Patient is formally released.
     - If **Treatment Unsuccessful**, streamline to:
       - **Diagnosis** (only if absolutely necessary) for quick reassessment and further treatment, minimizing rework. 

### Decision Points with Clear Criteria and Outcomes
- **Outcomes of Treatment**:
  - If treatment is **Successful**: Proceed to discharge directly, ensuring a quick transition for the patient.
  - If treatment is **Unsuccessful**: Assess need for diagnosis but minimize extensive loops and rework unless absolutely warranted based on patient condition. Regular feedback loops for the medical staff ensure timely adjustments.

### Parallel Activities and Synchronization Points
- Both **Register at FD** and **Register at ER** can occur simultaneously, allowing flexibility in patient inflow while ensuring that patients are quickly moved to the examination phase. This parallel processing reduces bottlenecks and unnecessary delays.

### Streamlined Exception Handling
- Upon **Treatment Unsuccessful**, a rapid feedback mechanism prompts the examination results to be rapidly evaluated, ensuring that adjustments to treatment can be made in an efficient manner, thus avoiding lengthy repetitive loops.

### End Conditions and Outcomes
The process is completed successfully when patients are discharged after receiving effective treatment. Confirmation of a successful outcome relies on:
- Patient satisfaction feedback and post-discharge assessments indicating recovery.

## Expected Process Metrics and Performance Improvements
- Enhanced patient throughput is expected, with significant reductions in average treatment and discharge times which should ideally fall below 40,000 milliseconds due to reduced waiting periods.
- The process aims to increase successful treatment rates, thus improving overall patient outcomes.
- Minimizing rework and redundant examinations should result in improved operational efficiency, projected to increase from previous rates.

## Key Improvements Made Compared to the AS-IS Model
- Consolidated the examination and diagnosis phases into a more streamlined approach, significantly reducing the complexity of pathways and ensuring quick transitions.
- Eliminated unnecessary rework loops, preserving resources and improving patient satisfaction.
- Simplified decision points to create a more direct and faster patient journey while allowing for rapid adjustments in care as needed.
- Optimized parallel execution of registration processes to prevent delays and bottlenecks.

This IDEAL process model emphasizes an improved and efficient delivery of healthcare services, enhancing patient satisfaction and operational excellence within the treatment workflow.
