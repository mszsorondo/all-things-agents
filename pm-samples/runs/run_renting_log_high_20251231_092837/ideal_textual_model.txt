### IDEAL Process Model

#### Process Overview and Purpose
The IDEAL process model streamlines the apartment rental lifecycle into a compliant, efficient flow. It enforces complete checks before approvals, eliminates bypasses/skips, mandates full screening, simplifies post-move-in rent handling with clear escalation for non-payment, and reduces rejection points. The purpose remains tenant acquisition and management, but with governance: early filtering, consistent processes, and proactive issue resolution to minimize long problematic tenancies and operational waste.

#### Key Stakeholders and Roles Involved
Roles not specified in sources; implies rental agency (scheduling, screening, collections) and tenants.

#### Detailed Step-by-Step Process Flow
**Start Conditions and Triggers:** Prospective tenant submits application → **Apply for Viewing Appointment**.

1. **Apply for Viewing Appointment** → **Set Appointment**.
2. **Set Appointment** → **View The Property**.
3. **View The Property** → **Hand In The Paperwork**.
4. **Hand In The Paperwork** → **Check Paperwork**.
5. **Check Paperwork** → **Screen Prospective Tenant**.
6. **Screen Prospective Tenant** → **Extensive Screening** (mandatory for viable candidates).
7. **Extensive Screening** → Decision Gateway: Suitability check.
   - Reject → **Reject Prospective Tenant** → End.
   - Approve → **Sign Contract** → **Move In**.

8. Post-Move In - Structured Monthly Rent Loop:
   - **Move In** → **Pay Rent** (monthly trigger).
   - After Pay Rent: Loop back to next **Pay Rent** (ongoing tenancy).
   - If **Miss Rent Payment** (detected): → **Issue Warning**.
   - **Issue Warning** → Decision: Pays late?
     - Yes → **Accept Late Payment** → back to **Pay Rent** loop.
     - No (second miss) → **Evict Tenant** → End.
   - At any tenancy point: Tenant may **Tenant Cancels Apartment** → End.

**Decision Points and Gateways:**
- Single Reject Gateway post-Extensive Screening (criteria: full background/credit check passes).
- Post-Warning XOR: Late pay accepted (one chance) vs. Evict (enforce after repeat miss).
- Inclusive optional: Tenant Cancel at any post-move-in point.

**Parallel Activities and Synchronization Points:** None; fully sequential with loops.

**Exception Handling and Error Paths:**
- Early reject only after full screening (no pre-screen skips).
- Non-payment: Strict 1-warning policy → Evict.

**Rework Loops and Escalation Procedures:**
- Application: Linear, no loops.
- Tenancy: Simple Pay Rent self-loop; Miss → Warn → Late Accept (back to loop) or Evict.
- Escalation: Auto-evict after one missed post-warning payment.

**End Conditions and Outcomes:**
- Reject Prospective Tenant (post-full checks).
- Evict Tenant (non-payment escalation).
- Tenant Cancels Apartment (voluntary exit).

#### Process Metrics and Performance Characteristics
- Reduced cycle time: Mandatory full application flow (~300-400k ms estimated vs. variable AS-IS).
- Fewer variants: 3 main paths (reject, successful tenancy short/long, evict).
- Target: 80%+ move-in success post-screen; average tenancy 12 months (12 Pays); <5% evictions.
- Performance: Streamlined steps cut screening time; automated warnings.

#### Key Improvements Made
- Eliminated skips/bypasses: Ensures consistent, compliant screening (no early rejections without cause).
- Single rejection point: Reduces rework, catches issues earlier but after investment.
- Simplified rent handling: Clear escalation prevents endless late payment loops; promotes timely payments.
- Removed redundant paths: Extensive always after initial screen; no optional screening.
- Bottleneck fixes: Sequential enforcement speeds decisions.
- Better governance: Limits long low-value tenancies, increases turnover for better tenants.
- Overall: Higher efficiency, lower rejection waste, enforceable policies.
