# AS-IS Process Model

## Process Overview and Purpose
The purpose of the process is to manage the rental application and tenancy operations for prospective tenants in a property management context. It includes steps from applying for a viewing appointment to signing the contract, moving in, and managing rental payments, including scenarios of payment issues and tenant cancellations.

## Key Stakeholders and Roles Involved
- **Prospective Tenant:** Initiates the process by applying for property viewing.
- **Property Management Staff:** Responsible for screening tenants, handling paperwork, and enforcing lease agreements.
- **Tenant:** Once approved, manages rental payments and responsibilities associated with tenancy. 

## Detailed Step-by-Step Process Flow

### Start Conditions and Triggers
The process begins with a prospective tenant initiating an application for viewing a property. Specifically, the start event is signaled by:
- **Apply for Viewing Appointment**

### Sequence Flow of Activities
1. **Apply for Viewing Appointment:** A prospective tenant applies to view the property.
2. **Set Appointment:** Property management staff sets a viewing appointment.
3. **View The Property:** The prospective tenant views the property.
4. **Hand In The Paperwork:** After viewing, the tenant submits the necessary applications and documents.
5. **Check Paperwork:** Property management reviews submitted paperwork for completeness and accuracy.
6. **Screen Prospective Tenant:** The prospective tenant undergoes screening.
   - **Decision Point:**
     - **If screened successfully:** Move to "Sign Contract"
     - **If rejected:** Move to "Reject Prospective Tenant."

### Decision Points with Criteria and Possible Outcomes
- **Screening Outcomes:**
  - **Successful Screening → Sign Contract → Move In**
  - **Unsuccessful Screening → Reject Prospective Tenant**
  
### Exception Handling and Error Paths
- **Tenant Cancels Apartment:** If at any point during the viewing or after signing, the tenant decides to cancel the apartment, the process is routed to "Tenant Cancels Apartment," leading directly to the end of the process.
- **Miss Rent Payment:** If the tenant fails to make a rent payment:
  - Move to "Issue Warning."
  - Subsequent actions can include accepting late payments or evicting the tenant, depending on compliance actions taken by property management.

### Rework Loops and Escalation Procedures
- **Payment Issues:**
  - After issuing a warning for missed payments, the outcomes can entail either accepting a late payment or proceeding toward eviction.
  - If a late payment is accepted, the flow returns back to "Pay Rent."
  
- **Rework Scenario:**
  - In cases where issues are found in the paperwork, it may lead to redoing the "Hand In The Paperwork" or "Check Paperwork" steps.

### End Conditions and Outcomes
The process concludes once the tenant either:
- **Successfully moves in after signing the contract.**
- **Has their application rejected.**
- **Cancels their apartment request.**
- **Faces eviction due to continuous payment issues.**

## Process Metrics and Performance Characteristics
- High frequency of missed rent payments with performance indicating potential issues in tenant commitment.
- Variants of the process indicate significant pathways including cancellations, rejections, and multiple re-payment attempts.

## Key Challenges and Pain Points Observed in the Current Process
- **High Missed Payment Rate:** Frequent instances where tenants fail to pay rent, leading to warnings and potential evictions.
- **Cancellations:** Regular cancellation incidents which indicate possible dissatisfaction or issues in the property showing or tenant expectations.
- **Decision Overlap:** The extensive branching from the screening and paperwork checks can lead to delays and inefficiencies.

This AS-IS model comprehensively represents the observed behaviors in the current operational process, capturing all variants and paths as evidenced in the data provided.

# IDEAL Process Model

## Process Overview and Purpose
The improved process aims to streamline the rental application and tenancy management, enhancing efficiency and ensuring compliance. This optimized flow reduces delays, eliminates unnecessary rework, and aligns actions for better governance. The goal is to create a smoother experience for prospective tenants while ensuring thorough vetting and accountability in property management.

## Key Stakeholders and Roles Involved
- **Prospective Tenant:** Initiates the application process for a rental property.
- **Property Management Staff:** Responsible for managing the entire rental application process, including screening, compliance, and documentation.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Process Flow

### Start Conditions and Triggers
The process is initiated when a prospective tenant expresses interest in renting a property by:
- **Apply for Viewing Appointment**

### Optimized Sequence of Activities
1. **Apply for Viewing Appointment:** The prospective tenant submits their request to view the property.
2. **Set Appointment:** Property management staff confirm and set a viewing appointment.
3. **View The Property:** The prospective tenant completes the property viewing.
4. **Hand In The Paperwork:** The prospective tenant submits the necessary application paperwork immediately after the property viewing.
5. **Check Paperwork & Screen Prospective Tenant (Parallel):**
   - **Check Paperwork:** Ensure all submitted documents are accurate and complete.
   - **Screen Prospective Tenant:** Assess suitability based on the application and background checks simultaneously to reduce waiting time.
     - **Decision Point:**
       - **If screened successfully:** Proceed to "Sign Contract."
       - **If rejected:** Move to "Reject Prospective Tenant."

### Decision Points with Criteria and Possible Outcomes
- **Screening Outcomes:**
  - **Successful Screening → Sign Contract → Move In**
  - **Unsuccessful Screening → Reject Prospective Tenant (with direct feedback on reasons for rejection)**

### Streamlined Exception Handling
- **Missed Payments:** 
  - Upon the tenant's first missed payment, an automatic notification is sent to remind them of the payment due.
  - If a payment is still missed after the reminder, issue a warning with a specified timeframe for resolution.
  - If unresolved, proceed to discuss potential evictions respectfully, allowing for any valid reasons by scheduling a discussion with property management.

### End Conditions and Outcomes
The process concludes once the tenant either:
- **Successfully moves in after signing the contract.**
- **Has their application rejected, with clear communication regarding any rejection reasons.**
- **Provides necessary explanations or documents for payment issues without excessive penalty.**

## Expected Process Metrics and Performance Improvements
- **Reduction in Processing Time:** Parallel handling of paperwork checks and screenings is expected to drastically cut down on the overall process duration.
- **Lower Missed Payment Rates:** Improved communication and faster follow-up processes for missed payments will likely lead to reduced occurrences of late payments.
- **Higher Conversion Rates:** A more seamless application and approval process should increase the acceptance rates of prospective tenants, leading to higher occupancy rates.

## Key Improvements Made Compared to the AS-IS Model
- **Parallelization of Checks:** Checking paperwork and screening the prospective tenant occur simultaneously, eliminating unnecessary delays.
- **Streamlined Communication for Payment Issues:** Implementing automated notifications and clear communication paths for rejections and missed payments reduces ambiguity and enhances tenant relations.
- **Eliminated Redundant Steps:** Reduced unnecessary rework loops, particularly around paperwork handling and screening processes.
- **Integrated Feedback Mechanisms:** Clear instructions and reasons are provided for any rejections or warnings to foster a transparent relationship between tenants and property management.

This IDEAL process model reflects an optimized version of the original process, addressing inefficiencies, enhancing cooperation, and fostering a smooth experience for all parties involved while maintaining core business objectives.
