# IDEAL Process Model

## Process Overview and Purpose
The IDEAL process model aims to streamline the recruitment workflow to enhance candidate experience while maintaining thorough evaluation measures. It seeks to reduce unnecessary rework, eliminate bottlenecks, and ensure timely decision-making throughout the process.

## Key Stakeholders and Roles Involved
- **Candidates**: Individuals applying for the job, whose experience should be prioritized.
- **Recruiters**: Responsible for managing the application process and conducting initial screenings.
- **Interviewers**: Employees tasked with evaluating candidates, focusing on reducing the number of interviews.
- **HR Managers**: Final decision-makers on hiring who also oversee the adherence to the streamlined process.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Process Flow
1. **Start Condition**: The process begins when a candidate **Hand In Job Application**.

2. **Initial Screening**: Conduct a **Telephonic Screening** immediately after receipt of an application to determine fit.
   - Candidates may proceed to a **Background Check** directly if they qualify.
   - Rejections must be communicated effectively post-screening.

3. **Background Check**: Implement a single comprehensive background check that feeds into all subsequent interviews. 
   - Positive results lead to **Coding Interview** and **Behavioral Interview**.

4. **Unified Interviewing Process**: Combine **Screening Interview**, **Frontend** or **Backend Interviews**, and **Coding Interviews** into one streamlined series, assessed in parallel to optimize time. 
   - This step efficiently filters candidates based on performance.

5. **Final Decision**: After the interviewing stage, candidates receive a consolidated decision based on performance across all interviews:
   - Candidates either receive a **Make Job Offer** or **Application Rejected**.

6. **End Conditions**: The process ends when an offer is made or an application is rejected, with a clear communication of the outcome and next steps provided to every candidate.

## Decision Points and Criteria
- Decision points include a single background check result influencing all interviews, thereby simplifying the flow.
- Clear criteria for rejections based on individual interviews or checks enables better candidate fit to job specifications.

## Parallel Activities and Synchronization Points
- Conduct interviews in parallel to maximize candidates’ time and minimize waiting periods, leading to quicker decisions.

## Exception Handling and Error Paths
- In cases where a candidate is rejected post-background check, communicate directly and promptly, offering feedback on why they did not progress.

## Process Metrics and Performance Characteristics
- By reducing the number of required interviews and merging background checks, leading to faster overall completion times, thus improving candidate satisfaction.

## Key Improvements Made
- Streamlined processes reduce excessive applicant screening steps, leading to significant time conservation.
- Enhanced communication protocols prevent candidate drop-offs and uncertainty due to prolonged wait times.
