Branch A: Decision/denial track

  Option B2: Direct submission without co-signer/collateral sub-work

  Option B3: Do prerequisite sub-work in parallel, then submit

Contract/signing stage and final synchronization

Key challenges and pain points
- Ambiguous concurrency vs decision semantics: After verification, the process splits into a denial track and a preparation track in parallel. This causes visible ordering where Submit may precede Loan Denied, which can confuse stakeholders and complicate controls.

- Fast-track signing without formal submission (skipped_examination): 332 cases bypass submission entirely; unless governed by clear criteria, this is a compliance and risk concern.
- Rework and variability in collateral assessment: Repeated visits (multi-visit loops) add delay and unpredictability.
- Optional co-signer and collateral activities are handled via invisible skips rather than explicit rules, making the process hard to audit and optimize.
- Lack of an explicit visible approval step before signing: Approval is inferred by the absence of denial and a silent completion of the denial branch, which weakens traceability.
- Long waits between early stages (e.g., appointment scheduling and intake) contribute to overall lead time.
