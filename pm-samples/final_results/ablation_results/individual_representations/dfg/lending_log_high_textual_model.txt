IDEAL Process Model

Process overview and purpose
- Purpose: Streamline and govern the credit application lifecycle to reduce delays, eliminate noncompliant shortcuts, minimize rework, and ensure decisions are made with complete, consistent information.
- Approach: Preserve the observed functional steps but enforce clear sequencing, inclusive decision logic for risk mitigations, controlled rework, and service-level expectations to address timing bottlenecks.

Key stakeholders and roles involved
- Applicant/Borrower: Requests appointment, submits application, signs agreement if approved.
- Underwriter: Performs final decisioning (approve -> proceed to signing; deny -> terminate).
- Co-signer: Provides support when explicitly required by risk criteria.
- Note: Other internal roles remain unspecified; responsibilities are described by activity without assigning additional role names.

Detailed step-by-step process flow (to-be)

1) Start conditions and triggers
- Trigger: Applicant submits Request Appointment.

2) Appointment handling (controlled timing)
- Decision: Appointment Approval.
  - If approved: Set Appointment within an SLA (target ≤ 8 hours from request).
  - If denied: Appointment Denied (end).
- Activity: Hand In Credit Application promptly after appointment (target ≤ 8 hours from appointment setting).

3) Verification (mandatory, standardized)
- Activity: Verify Borrowers Information using a defined checklist (documentation and data completeness enforced).
- Decision after verification (governed by explicit criteria):
  - Reject for non-eligibility: Application Rejected (end).
  - Proceed directly to underwriting (when criteria fully met and no mitigations required).
  - Require risk mitigations:
    - Request Co-Signer On Loan (when debt-to-income/creditworthiness thresholds indicate need).
    - Make Visit to Assess Collateral (when collateralized loan requires on-site assessment).
  - Note: The path "skipped_examination" is removed in the IDEAL model; all signings require either a completed underwriting decision or a defined, compliant fast-track that still includes underwriting approval.

4) Risk mitigations (inclusive, ordered or parallelizable, with clear completion rules)
- Inclusive gateway: One or both of the following may be required:
  - Request Co-Signer On Loan.
  - Make Visit to Assess Collateral.
- Execution:
  - The two activities may be performed in any order; where feasible, they may proceed in parallel to reduce cycle time.
  - Completion criteria:
    - Co-signer: signed and verified co-signer commitment on file.
