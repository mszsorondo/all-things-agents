Process overview and purpose
- Purpose: Deliver patient care from first registration through diagnosis, treatment, and discharge.
- Scope captured by the event-log variants: Two entry points (Register at FD or Register at ER), one or more clinical examinations (Examination, Expert Examination, and/or Thorough Examination), Diagnosis, Treatment, outcome assessment (Treatment successful or Treatment unsuccessful), possible re-diagnosis and re-treatment loops, and Discharge.

  - Registration (FD or ER)
  - Clinical staff conducting Examination
  - Clinical specialist(s) conducting Expert Examination
  - Diagnostic services performing Thorough Examination
  - Clinician(s) establishing Diagnosis and providing Treatment
  - Discharge/administrative staff completing Discharge
- Patient is the subject of the process.

- Trigger: Patient presents for care and is registered at exactly one of:

  - Register at ER

- Treatment: Therapeutic intervention.
