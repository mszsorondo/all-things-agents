Business Process Description:
# AS-IS Process Model

## Process Overview and Purpose
This AS-IS process model represents a residential property rental management process, covering the complete lifecycle from prospective tenant application through tenancy management to termination. The process includes tenant screening, contract signing, move-in procedures, rent payment management, and various termination scenarios including eviction and tenant-initiated cancellations.

## Key Stakeholders and Roles Involved
* **Prospective Tenants**: Individuals applying to rent properties
* **Property Management Staff**: Responsible for appointment scheduling, property viewings, and paperwork handling
* **Screening/Compliance Team**: Conducts tenant screening and background checks
* **Contract Management Team**: Handles contract signing and legal documentation
* **Rent Collection Team**: Manages rent payments and late payment handling
* **Tenant Relations Team**: Handles warnings, evictions, and tenant communications

## Detailed Step-by-Step Process Flow

### Start Conditions and Triggers
The process begins when a prospective tenant submits a viewing appointment application for a rental property.

### Process Activities/Tasks in Sequence

#### Phase 1: Application and Viewing Process
1. **Apply for Viewing Appointment**: Prospective tenant submits application to view a property
2. **Set Appointment**: Property management schedules viewing appointment
3. **View The Property**: Prospective tenant tours the property with management staff

#### Phase 2: Screening and Decision Process
4. **Hand In The Paperwork**: Prospective tenant submits required documentation
5. **Check Paperwork**: Management verifies completeness and accuracy of submitted documents
6. **Screen Prospective Tenant**: Initial screening assessment
   * **Decision Point**: Based on initial screening results
     * Path A: Proceed to "Extensive Screening" (more thorough background check)
     * Path B: Directly to "Sign Contract" (if initial screening is sufficient)
     * Path C: "Reject Prospective Tenant" (if screening fails)

7. **Extensive Screening** (Optional): Additional background checks and verification
   * **Decision Point**: Based on extensive screening results
     * Path A: "Sign Contract" (if screening passes)
     * Path B: "Reject Prospective Tenant" (if screening fails)

#### Phase 3: Contract and Move-In
8. **Sign Contract**: Both parties execute rental agreement
9. **Move In**: Tenant takes possession of the property

#### Phase 4: Tenancy Management - Rent Payment Cycle
10. **Pay Rent**: Tenant submits monthly rent payment
    * **Looping Behavior**: The process shows significant looping where "Pay Rent" can repeat multiple times before proceeding
    * **Decision Point**: After rent payment
      * Path A: Continue to next "Pay Rent" cycle (most frequent path)
      * Path B: "Tenant Cancels Apartment" (tenant initiates termination)
      * Path C: "Miss Rent Payment" (payment failure)

#### Phase 5: Late Payment Handling
11. **Miss Rent Payment**: Tenant fails to pay rent on time
12. **Issue Warning**: Management issues formal warning for late payment
    * **Decision Point**: After issuing warning
      * Path A: "Accept Late Payment" (tenant pays after warning)
      * Path B: "Evict Tenant" (proceed to eviction if payment not received)

13. **Accept Late Payment**: Management accepts late payment with possible penalties
    * **Decision Point**: After accepting late payment
      * Path A: Return to "Pay Rent" cycle
      * Path B: Return to "Miss Rent Payment" (if payment issues continue)

#### Phase 6: Process Termination
14. **Reject Prospective Tenant**: Application rejected during screening phase
15. **Tenant Cancels Apartment**: Tenant voluntarily terminates tenancy
16. **Evict Tenant**: Management initiates legal eviction process

### Decision Points with Criteria and Possible Outcomes
1. **After Initial Screening**: 
   * Criteria: Initial screening results and risk assessment
   * Outcomes: Extensive Screening (higher risk), Sign Contract (low risk), Reject (failed screening)

2. **After Extensive Screening**:
   * Criteria: Comprehensive background check results
   * Outcomes: Sign Contract (pass), Reject (fail)

3. **After Rent Payment**:
   * Criteria: Tenant's continued tenancy and payment behavior
   * Outcomes: Continue tenancy, Tenant cancels, Miss payment

4. **After Issuing Warning**:
   * Criteria: Tenant response to warning and payment compliance
   * Outcomes: Accept late payment, Evict tenant

### Parallel Activities and Synchronization Points
The process is primarily sequential with decision-based branching rather than true parallel execution. However, the rent payment cycle represents a recurring parallel activity that continues independently of other process flows.

### Exception Handling and Error Paths
* **Early Rejection Paths**: Multiple points where prospective tenants can be rejected:
  - Immediately after application (849 cases)
  - After property viewing (1,710 cases)
  - After initial screening (2,856 cases)
  - After extensive screening (1,815 cases)

* **Payment Failure Paths**: 
  - Direct from "Pay Rent" to "Miss Rent Payment" (770 cases)
  - From "Accept Late Payment" back to "Miss Rent Payment" (61 cases)
  - From "Move In" directly to "Miss Rent Payment" (114 cases)

### Rework Loops and Escalation Procedures
* **Rent Payment Loop**: Significant evidence of multiple rent payment cycles (up to 22 consecutive payments observed)
* **Late Payment Escalation**: Three-step escalation: Miss Payment → Issue Warning → (Accept Late Payment or Evict)
* **Screening Escalation**: Two-tier screening with optional extensive screening for higher-risk applicants

### End Conditions and Outcomes
The process terminates in one of three ways:
1. **Application Rejection**: Prospective tenant rejected during screening phase
2. **Voluntary Termination**: Tenant cancels apartment (2,680 cases)
3. **Eviction**: Legal eviction process (90 cases)

## Process Metrics and Performance Characteristics
* **Total Process Variants**: 30+ distinct process paths observed
* **Most Frequent Path**: Application → Screening → Rejection (2,856 cases, 406,411 seconds average duration)
* **Successful Tenancy Paths**: Multiple variants with extensive rent payment cycles
* **Performance Issues**: 
  - Extensive screening adds significant time (506,227 seconds vs 406,411 seconds for basic rejection)
  - Long tenancies show extreme duration (up to 1,774,677 seconds for 15+ payment cycles)
* **Conversion Rates**: 
  - 2,770 successful move-ins from 9,151 applications (30.3% conversion)
  - 5,466 rejections (59.7% rejection rate)
  - 915 cases terminated early without viewing

## Key Challenges and Pain Points
1. **Excessive Process Variants**: 30+ different paths indicate inconsistent process execution
2. **High Rejection Rates**: Nearly 60% of applicants are rejected, suggesting either overly strict screening or poor applicant targeting
3. **Lengthy Screening Process**: Extensive screening adds approximately 100,000 seconds (27.8 hours) to process time
4. **Payment Issues**: Significant late payment problems (770 direct misses, plus escalations)
5. **Tenant Turnover**: High cancellation rates (2,680 cases) indicate potential tenant dissatisfaction
6. **Process Inefficiency**: Multiple points where applicants can be rejected late in the process after significant time investment
7. **Eviction Process**: Although rare (90 cases), evictions represent process failure and legal complications
8. **Performance Variability**: Extreme duration differences between simple rejections (85,701 seconds) and long tenancies (1.7M+ seconds)
