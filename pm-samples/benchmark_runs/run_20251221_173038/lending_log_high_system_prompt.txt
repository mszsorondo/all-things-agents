  - Trigger: Borrower initiates an appointment request.

    - "Appointment Denied" (observed frequency 962): appointment is denied and the case ends.

- Step 4: Request Co-Signer On Loan and Make Visit to Assess Collateral (interactions and loops)

- Step 5: skipped_examination path

This indicates an observed bypass of normal preparatory steps in some cases, where an agreement is signed without a documented submission-to-underwriter transition in the log.

- Step 6: Submit File to Underwriter -> Outcome

5. Decision points with criteria (as observed/inferred)
- Appointment denial: criteria not present in the log; 962 cases end here. Likely early-screening negative outcome.
- Verification branching: based on verification results, the process may:
  - go to underwriter submission if the application appears complete and acceptable,
  - be rejected immediately if verification fails (1624 cases),
  - need a co-signer or collateral inspection, possibly both. The data shows these are sometimes combined and can be repeated.
- Underwriter decision: the underwriter either approves (leading to signing) or denies the loan. High denial frequency (4787 observed direct denies from submission) suggests strict underwriting or poor upstream checks.
