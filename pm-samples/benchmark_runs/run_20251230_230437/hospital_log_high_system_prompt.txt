# AS-IS Process Model: Healthcare Treatment Process

## Process Overview and Purpose
This AS-IS process model represents a healthcare treatment workflow for patients requiring medical attention. The process begins with patient registration at either the Emergency Room (ER) or Family Department (FD), proceeds through various examination and diagnostic stages, provides treatment, and concludes with either successful discharge or treatment continuation. The process exhibits significant variability, rework loops, and multiple pathways based on patient condition and examination outcomes.

## Key Stakeholders and Roles Involved
- **Patients**: Individuals seeking medical treatment
- **ER Registration Staff**: Personnel handling emergency room registrations
- **FD Registration Staff**: Personnel handling family department registrations
- **Medical Examiners**: Healthcare professionals conducting initial examinations
- **Expert Examiners**: Specialists conducting thorough or specialized examinations
- **Diagnosticians**: Medical professionals responsible for diagnosis
- **Treatment Providers**: Healthcare staff administering treatments
- **Discharge Coordinators**: Personnel managing patient discharge

- **Trigger**: Patient arrives seeking medical attention

  1. Emergency Room (ER) registration
