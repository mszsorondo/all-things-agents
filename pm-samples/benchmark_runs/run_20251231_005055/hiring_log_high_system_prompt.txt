# AS-IS Process Model

## Process Overview and Purpose
The AS-IS process represents the current hiring and recruitment workflow for technical positions within the organization. This process handles candidate applications from initial submission through various screening, interviewing, and background check stages, culminating in either job offers or rejections. The process exhibits significant complexity with multiple parallel tracks, rework loops, and high variability in execution paths.

## Key Stakeholders and Roles Involved
* **Candidates**: Individuals submitting job applications
* **Recruitment Team**: Responsible for initial screening and coordination
* **Hiring Managers**: Conduct technical interviews and make hiring decisions
- **Background Check Team**: Performs standard and extensive background verifications
* **Technical Interviewers**: Specialists conducting domain-specific interviews (Frontend, Backend, ML, Coding)
* **HR Department**: Manages offer letters and final hiring documentation

## Detailed Step-by-Step Process Flow

1. **Process Trigger**: A candidate submits a job application through the company's career portal or recruitment system
2. **Initial Event**: "Hand In Job Application" - The candidate completes and submits their application materials

6. **Extensive Background Check** (Conditional): Additional verification for certain candidates
