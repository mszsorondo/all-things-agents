Process overview and purpose
- Scope: Residential rental lifecycle from prospecting (viewing request) through tenant onboarding and ongoing rent collection, including exceptions for cancellations, rejections, late payments, and evictions.
- Purpose: Describe how prospects become tenants and how rent is subsequently collected, reflecting observed behavior in the event log.

Key stakeholders and roles involved

- Real Estate Agents (Real Estate Agent 1–5): Set appointments, conduct/host property viewings, perform initial screening of prospective tenants, communicate rejections.

IDEAL Process Model

Process overview and purpose
- Scope: Streamlined residential rental lifecycle that reduces cancellations and rejections, accelerates qualified applicants to contract, and enforces disciplined rent exception handling.
- Purpose: Reorder and synchronize existing activities, eliminate wasteful late-stage screening, and tighten exception management while maintaining the same set of operational tasks observed in the log.

Key stakeholders and roles involved
- Tenant: Applies, provides documents, attends viewing, signs contract, moves in, pays rent.
- Hotline: Supports appointment coordination as needed.
- Real Estate Agents (1–5): Pre-qualify applicants, schedule and conduct viewings, communicate decisions.
- Lawyers (1–3): Perform Extensive Screening only when flagged; manage evictions.
- Landlords (1–5): Approve final decisions, sign contracts, issue warnings, accept late payments.

Detailed step-by-step process flow

Start conditions and triggers
- Trigger: Tenant signals interest in a property.

Front-loaded qualification to reduce waste

2) Hand In The Paperwork (Tenant)

3) Parallel early checks (synchronized before scheduling)

4) Conditional deep-dive
   - Extensive Screening (Lawyers) only if triggered by the early checks.
   - Synchronization point: Finalize outcomes of any extensive screening prior to scheduling.

Decision to proceed or decline
