### AS-IS Process Model

#### Process Overview and Purpose
The AS-IS process model captures the observed behavior of a loan application process as discovered from event logs using the inductive miner (Petri net), directly-follows graph (DFG), and abstract process variants. This process handles customer requests for credit loans, involving appointment scheduling, application submission and verification, optional co-signer requests and collateral assessments (with significant looping and interleaving), submission to underwriter, and final outcomes such as approval (signing agreement), denials, or rejections. The process exhibits high variability, with 25+ variants, frequent rework loops, multiple denial points, and skipping of examinations, leading to inefficiencies and compliance risks.

#### Key Stakeholders and Roles Involved
Roles are not explicitly specified in the input models; activities are performed by unspecified loan processing staff, verifiers, underwriters, and collateral assessors.

#### Detailed Step-by-Step Process Flow
**Start Conditions and Triggers**: The process starts when a customer submits a 'Request Appointment' for a loan application (initial marking at 'source').

1. **Request Appointment**: Customer requests an appointment to apply for a loan.
   
2. **Decision Point: Appointment Approval** (Exclusive Gateway):
   - Criteria: Unspecified business rules (e.g., initial eligibility).
   - Outcomes:
     - Yes (frequency ~90%): Proceed to 'Set Appointment'.
     - No (frequency 962 cases): 'Appointment Denied' → End (process terminates at 'sink').

3. **Set Appointment**: Schedule the appointment with the customer.

4. **Hand In Credit Application**: Customer submits the credit application during the appointment.

5. **Verify Borrowers Information**: Staff verifies the borrower's details and information.
   
6. **Decision Point: Verification Outcome** (Exclusive Gateway):
   - Criteria: Information accuracy and completeness.
   - Outcomes:
     - Fail (frequency 1624 cases): 'Application Rejected' → End ('sink').
     - Pass (~80% of cases post-appointment): Proceed to post-verification split.

7. **Post-Verification Split** (Inclusive/Parallel Gateway via tauSplit_1):
   - Splits into multiple optional branches, allowing combinations:
     - **Early Denial Branch** (p7):
       - 'Loan Denied' → End ('sink' via p8 and tauJoin_2).
       - Or skip denial → direct end ('sink').
     - **Main Processing Branch** (p9, with tauSplit_5 for options):
       - **Direct Skip Examination** (frequency 332 cases): 'skipped_examination' → 'Sign Loan Agreement' → End (Approved, 'sink' via p10 and tauJoin_2).
       - **Submit Path** (via p12): 'Submit File to Underwriter' → p11 → either:
         - 'Sign Loan Agreement' (Approved, frequency 2295 post-submit) → p10 → End ('sink').
         - Skip sign (skip_13, frequency 4787 post-submit 'Loan Denied' behavior) → p10 → End (Denied, 'sink').
       - **Optional Co-Signer and Collateral Branches** (interleaved, via tauSplit_5 → p13/p15, merged at tauJoin_6 → p12 → Submit):
         - **Co-Signer**: 'Request Co-Signer On Loan' (p13 → p14) or skip → merge. Frequency 1233 initiations, can interleave.
         - **Collateral Assessment Loop** (p15 → init_loop_9 → p17): 'Make Visit to Assess Collateral' (p18) → loop back (skip_11 → p17, frequency 563 self-loops) or exit (skip_12 → p16 → merge). Loops up to 13+ times in rare variants (e.g., frequency 1 case), can interleave with co-signer (e.g., Make Visit → Request Co-Signer freq 539, vice versa 733).

**Synchronization Points**: 
- tauJoin_6 merges co-signer/collateral paths before 'Submit File to Underwriter'.
- tauJoin_2 merges post-sign/denial paths to 'sink'.
- Loops primarily on collateral assessments, with interleaving of co-signer requests.

**Exception Handling and Error Paths**: Multiple denials (Appointment Denied, Application Rejected, Loan Denied early/post-submit). No explicit escalations; deviations handled via skips and loops.

**Rework Loops and Escalation Procedures**: 
- Heavy looping on 'Make Visit to Assess Collateral' (self-loop freq 563, up to 13+ iterations).
- Interleaving of 'Request Co-Signer On Loan' and collateral visits creates rework variants (e.g., co-signer → collateral → co-signer).
- No formal escalations; loops represent iterative assessments.

**End Conditions and Outcomes**:
- **Approved**: 'Sign Loan Agreement' (total ~2500 cases, including direct skip exam and post-submit).
- **Denied**: 'Loan Denied' (high frequency ~4700+ post-submit, plus early), 'Appointment Denied', or implicit skips.
- **Rejected**: 'Application Rejected' (1624 cases).
- All converge to 'sink'.

#### Process Metrics and Performance Characteristics
- Total observed cases: ~10,000 (based on Request Appointment freq 9038 + 962 denied).
- Variant diversity: 25+ variants, top 3 cover ~62% (direct denied, approved, rejected).
- High denial rate: ~50%+ cases end denied (Loan Denied dominant).
- Cycle times: Average 80,000–400,000 ms per edge; long paths with loops exceed 400k ms (e.g., 469k ms rare case).
- Bottlenecks: Loops add significant time (collateral self-loop perf 22k ms); Submit to Denied perf 82k ms.

#### Key Challenges and Pain Points
- **Excessive Rework**: Multiple iterations of collateral assessments (up to 13+) and interleaved co-signer requests cause delays and resource waste.
- **High Variability**: 25+ paths, including rare long loops, complicate execution and compliance.
- **Multiple Denial Points**: Inconsistent decision making (appointment, verify, early post-verify, post-submit).
- **Skips and Shortcuts**: 'skipped_examination' allows approvals without full checks (332 cases); skips lead to unclear outcomes.
- **Interleaving Complexity**: Optional branches allow unpredictable orders (e.g., co-signer before/after collateral).
- **Performance**: Long cycle times due to loops; high denial rates indicate poor filtering early.
- **Compliance Risks**: Loops suggest incomplete assessments; no clear governance on iterations.
