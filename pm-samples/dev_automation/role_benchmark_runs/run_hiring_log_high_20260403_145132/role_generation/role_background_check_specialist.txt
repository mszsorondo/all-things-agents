AS-IS Process Model — Background Check Specialist
============================================================

Title: AS-IS Process Model — Background Check Specialist

Section 1: Role Overview (purpose, responsibilities)
- Purpose: Execute initial and extensive background verifications and escalate to external providers when needed.
- Responsibilities: perform Background Check, initiate Extensive Background Check (directly or via deepResearch Inc.), and communicate results to HR Screener or Senior Partner; in some cases mark Application Rejected based on background findings.

Section 2: Activities Performed (complete list with descriptions)
- Background Check: routine verification of candidate credentials and basic checks.
- Extensive Background Check: deeper investigation, sometimes outsourced to deepResearch Inc.
- Application Rejected: close cases where background issues disqualify the candidate.

Section 3: Process Flow (when activated, what happens, decision points)
- Activation: Background Check Specialist is typically activated after Telephonic Screening (Telephonic Screening -> Background Check is frequent) or after Screening Interview (sometimes Background Check happens after Screening Interview).
- Flow choices observed (AS-IS):
  - Telephonic Screening -> Background Check -> Screening Interview -> Coding Interview
  - Telephonic Screening -> Screening Interview -> Background Check -> Extensive Background Check -> ...
  - Background Check -> Application Rejected (observed in many cases)
  - Background Check -> Extensive Background Check -> Coding Interview (if passed)
- Decision points handled:
  - After Background Check: either escalate to Extensive Background Check, forward to Screening Interview, forward to technical interviews (Coding Interview), or reject.
  - For Extensive Background Check: decide if external vendor (deepResearch Inc.) is required; vendor handoffs observed.

Section 4: Interactions (who hands work to this role, who receives work from this role)
- Inputs to Background Check Specialist: Telephonic Screening, Screening Interview, HR-pro (occasionally), and sometimes direct from HR-dummy.
- Outputs from Background Check Specialist:
  - Screening Interview (return to HR Screener),
  - Extensive Background Check (internal or outsourced to deepResearch Inc.),
  - Application Rejected (close case),
  - Coding Interview (advance to technical evaluation),
  - Senior Partner and HR-pro (for escalations and decision-making).
- Observed handover weights: HR-dummy hands significant work to deepResearch Inc. and Coding Interviewer occasionally; background specialist also passes work back to HR-dummy.

Section 5: Variants and Exceptions (all observed paths)
- Observed variants involving background checks (AS-IS):
  - Background Check -> Screening Interview -> Coding Interview
  - Background Check -> Extensive Background Check -> Coding Interview
  - Background Check -> Application Rejected
  - Background Check -> Extensive Background Check -> Application Rejected
- Exceptions: Cases escalated to deepResearch Inc. for external checks; some background checks directly lead to rejection without further interviews.

Section 6: Key Observations (pain points, inefficiencies observed in the data)
- Background checks sometimes occur before screening interviews and sometimes after; inconsistent ordering likely causes delays.
- High frequency of escalation to Extensive Background Check with high performance times (long durations) indicates a bottleneck.
- Outsourcing to deepResearch Inc. occurs but is a low-volume path; handover delays to external vendor could be a source of long lead times.

(End of AS-IS description for Background Check Specialist)
