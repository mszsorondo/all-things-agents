IDEAL Process Model

Process overview and purpose
- Purpose: Define a governed, controlled tenancy process that preserves all legitimate business behaviors while removing wasteful or noncompliant paths and clarifying decision logic and loops.
- Scope: From initial application through decision, onboarding, recurring rent handling, and clear termination outcomes (including normal completion as an end condition).

Key stakeholders and roles involved
- Prospective Tenant: Initiates application, may view property, submits paperwork, moves in, pays rent, may cancel.
- Property Management/Landlord: Conducts appointments, checks paperwork, screens tenant, decides on rejection/acceptance, issues warnings, may accept late payments under policy, and may evict in accordance with policy.

Detailed step-by-step process flow

Start conditions and trigger
- Trigger: 'Apply for Viewing Appointment' by a prospective tenant.

Governed pre-tenancy flow (mandatory checks before acceptance)
1) Apply for Viewing Appointment
2) Optional viewing (policy-allowed but controlled):
   - If the tenant chooses to view first:
     2.1) Set Appointment
     2.2) View The Property
   - If viewing is skipped, the process continues but paperwork and screening remain mandatory before any acceptance.
3) Mandatory documentation and screening (must be completed before decision):
   3.1) Hand In The Paperwork (required)
   3.2) Check Paperwork (required)
   3.3) Screen Prospective Tenant (required)
   3.4) Conditional: Extensive Screening (performed only when risk criteria warrant; otherwise skipped)
4) Decision on candidate (exclusive choice after screening):
   - Reject Prospective Tenant (end state for pre-lease cases)
   - Or accept and proceed:
     4.1) Sign Contract
     4.2) Move In

Controlled rent cycle loop (one-and-only-one outcome per period)
5) For each rent period (repeat until tenancy ends):
   5.1) Period start: determine payment status by due date.
   5.2) Exclusive outcomes per period:
        - On-time payment path:
          a) Pay Rent (completes the period)
        - Missed payment path:
          a) Miss Rent Payment
          b) Issue Warning (includes due notice per policy)
          c) Decision: Accept Late Payment this period?
             - If yes: Accept Late Payment (completes the period)
             - If no: no acceptance recorded for the period
          d) Escalation rule: If cumulative missed payments/warnings exceed policy thresholds across periods, proceed to eviction (see termination). Thresholds are policy-defined; the activity used remains 'Evict Tenant'.
   5.3) Proceed to next period and repeat the cycle.

Termination outcomes (clear and complete)
- Pre-lease termination:
  - Reject Prospective Tenant (as above).
- Post–move-in termination (exclusive outcome for the whole tenancy):
  - Tenant Cancels Appartment (voluntary early termination).
  - Evict Tenant (involuntary termination following governed escalation).

- Viewing decision: Optional, does not bypass mandatory documentation and screening.
- Screening outcome: Exclusive choice to reject or accept; acceptance is permitted only after 3.1–3.3 are completed (and 3.4 if triggered).
- Rent-cycle decision: Exactly one per period:
  - Pay on time OR miss→warning→(optional acceptance). Both cannot occur in the same period; neither cannot occur.
- Eviction escalation: Based on policy thresholds (e.g., number of missed payments/warnings or age of arrears); when reached, terminate via 'Evict Tenant'.

Parallel activities and synchronization
- None required; the process remains sequential with clear gateways and a governed loop.

Exception handling and error paths
- Missed payment handling:
  - Issue Warning each time a period is missed.
  - Optionally Accept Late Payment once for that period if paid within grace terms.
  - If thresholds are exceeded over time, terminate via 'Evict Tenant'.
- Early termination by tenant:
  - Tenant Cancels Appartment at any time post–move-in per policy.
- Screening exceptions:
  - Extensive Screening performed conditionally; if adverse results, reject.

Rework loops and escalation procedures
- Rework is limited to the rent-cycle loop:
  - Exactly one outcome per period (on-time pay or miss-handling).
  - Late acceptance is at most once per missed period (no inner repeated cycles in the same period).
  - Escalation to eviction follows defined thresholds across periods (no indefinite warning loops).
- No rework loops exist in pre-tenancy beyond re-submission of paperwork as needed (within 'Check Paperwork' resolution).

End conditions and outcomes
