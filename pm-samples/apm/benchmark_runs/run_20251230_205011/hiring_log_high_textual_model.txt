### AS-IS Process Model

#### Process Overview and Purpose
The AS-IS process model depicts the current, observed behavior of a job application and recruitment process for technical positions (such as frontend, backend, and machine learning roles), as discovered from event logs using the inductive miner algorithm, supplemented by directly-follows graph (DFG) frequencies, performance times, and abstract process variants. This process starts with a candidate submitting a job application and proceeds through multiple screening, interview, and verification stages, often resulting in rejection at various points or, less frequently, progressing to a job offer. The model reveals significant variability, including skipping steps, out-of-order execution of activities (e.g., interviews before or after background checks), rework loops (e.g., repeating Behavioral Interviews or Make Job Offer), crossovers between technical interviews (e.g., Frontend ↔ ML ↔ Backend), and frequent early terminations via Application Rejected. Silent transitions (tau), skips, and loops in the Petri net indicate optional paths, concurrency options, and rework possibilities. The purpose is to select suitable candidates, but the execution shows inefficiencies, high rejection rates, and non-standard sequencing.

#### Key Stakeholders and Roles Involved
No specific roles or swimlanes are defined in the input models. Activities are performed by unspecified recruiters, interviewers, and verification teams interacting with applicants.

#### Detailed Step-by-Step Process Flow
**Start Conditions and Triggers**: The process triggers upon the candidate executing "Hand In Job Application" (initial marking at source place). This is the universal starting point (frequency in variants: all paths).

1. **Hand In Job Application** → Leads to place p_4.
   - Next: Either "Telephonic Screening" (frequency 8799 in DFG) → p_6, or direct skip to p_5 (via skip_2).
   - From p_6 (post-Telephonic): Either "tauSplit_4" branching to "Screening Interview" (p_10 → p_11) or "Background Check" (p_8 → p_9), or skip_3 → p_5.
   - Direct "Application Rejected" possible (frequency 1201, performance ~146s).

2. **Screening Phase** (concurrent/optional via tauSplit_4 and tauJoin_5 → p_7):
   - "Telephonic Screening" → "Screening Interview" (freq 3848) or "Background Check" (freq 3961).
   - "Screening Interview" → "Background Check" (freq 3848, 0 time) or "Coding Interview" (1517) or "Extensive Background Check" (1055) or "Application Rejected" (1389).
   - "Background Check" → "Screening Interview" (3961, 0 time), "Coding Interview" (1413), "Extensive Background Check" (1044), or "Application Rejected" (1391).
   - Merge at tauJoin_5 → p_7.

3. **Background Verification** (from p_7):
   - Branches: "Extensive Background Check" → p_12 (freq after Screening/Background), or skip_6 → p_12.
   - From p_12: "Coding Interview" → p_13 or skip_7 → tauSplit_9? Wait, p_12 → Coding Interview (ea68...) → p_13 → tauSplit_9 or skip_8.
   - Extensive often leads to "Application Rejected" (1125).

4. **Technical Interviews** (highly variant, from p_13/tauSplit_9 splitting to p_14/ML, p_16/Backend, p_18/Frontend; loops via init_loop_17/22/27):
   - tauSplit_9 (3-way split post-Coding): "ML Interview" (p_14), "Backend Interview" (p_16), "Frontend Interview" (p_18).
   - Loops/skips: e.g., p_20 → init_loop_17 → p_22 → Reject or skip_16 → p_21; similar for others.
   - Observed behaviors (DFG/variants):
     - Coding → Frontend (1513) ↔ ML (1304/878) ↔ Backend (701/487).
     - Crossovers: Backend → Frontend (487), Frontend → Backend (529), ML → Backend (121).
     - Rejections mid-tech: Coding Reject (386), Frontend Reject (653), etc.
   - Merge via tauJoin_10 → p_5 (loop back?).

5. **Behavioral and Final Stages** (from various post-tech places → tauJoin_15 → sink or loops):
   - Paths to "Behavioral Interview" (e.g., Frontend → Behavioral 853, ML → Behavioral 754, Backend → Behavioral 417).
   - Loops: Behavioral → Behavioral (1032, ~47ms self-loop), Application Rejected → Behavioral (740).
   - From Behavioral: "Make Job Offer" (1850) or Reject (914).
   - Offer loops: Make Job Offer → Make Job Offer (861, ~71ms).
   - Reject loops: Application Rejected → Rejected (654).
   - Late rejects/offers: e.g., post-Offer Reject (110), Reject → Offer (690).

**Decision Points and Gateways**:
- Exclusive choices at skips/tauSplits: e.g., do Screening or Background first (mutually observed in variants).
- Inclusive/optional: Tech interviews skippable, order flexible (e.g., Coding → ML or Frontend first).
- Rejections: Exclusive gateways after nearly every activity (frequent in DFG).

**Parallel Activities and Synchronization**:
- Early screenings (Telephonic → parallel-ish Screening + Background via tauSplit_4).
- Tech interviews appear concurrent/flexible (multi-way tauSplit_9), sync via tauJoin_10/15.

**Exception Handling and Error Paths**:
- "Application Rejected" at any stage (e.g., post-Application 1201, post-Screening 1389, post-Tech high freq).
- Sometimes continues post-Reject (Reject → Behavioral/Offer).

**Rework Loops and Escalation**:
- init_loop_* and skips enable rework: e.g., post-tech loop back to Reject or more interviews.
- Self-loops on Behavioral (1032) and Offer (861).
- Escalation: "Extensive Background Check" often triggers Reject.

**End Conditions and Outcomes**:
- Positive: Reach "Make Job Offer" → sink (low freq ~few % based on variants).
- Negative: "Application Rejected" → sink (dominant, ~80-90% paths).
- Final marking: sink:1.

#### Process Metrics and Performance Characteristics
- Total variants: 30+ abstract, high frequency early rejects (e.g., post-Screening/Background ~2700 cases).
- Avg performance: Rejects ~150-320s early, 400-450s mid-tech; successful paths ~530s.
- Bottlenecks: Background/Screening swaps (0 time, ordering issues), tech crossovers (e.g., Frontend-ML 80s), loops add minimal but indicate rework.
- Rejection rate: Very high (~93% inferred from frequencies), low throughput to Offer.

#### Key Challenges and Pain Points
- Extreme variability (30+ variants), leading to inconsistent candidate experience.
- Frequent early/mid-process rejections waste recruiter/interviewer time.
- Out-of-order activities (e.g., Background before/after Screening) indicate poor guidelines.
- Rework loops (repeating interviews/offers) suggest inadequate preparation or negotiations.
- Skips and silent paths hide true logic, risking compliance issues.
- No clear progression gates; tech interviews unordered, causing inefficiencies.
- High performance times for failed paths, low for successes (potential data skew).
