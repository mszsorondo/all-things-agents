### AS-IS Process Model

#### Process Overview and Purpose
The AS-IS process model represents the current state of a job recruitment process for technical positions, as discovered from process mining using an inductive miner Petri net, directly-follows graph (DFG), and abstract variants derived from an event log. The purpose is to evaluate job applicants through a series of screenings, background checks, and interviews to decide whether to make a job offer or reject the application. The process exhibits high variability, with numerous optional steps, parallel branches, skips, loops, and frequent rejections at multiple points, reflecting real-world deviations, rework, and inefficiencies observed in the event log.

#### Key Stakeholders and Roles Involved
- Job applicants (submit applications and participate in screenings/interviews).
- Recruiters/HR personnel (handle screenings, background checks, and offers).
- Interviewers (conduct technical and behavioral interviews).
No specific role assignments per activity are detailed in the source models; activities are performed by relevant recruitment team members as needed.

#### Detailed Step-by-Step Process Flow
**Start Conditions and Triggers:** The process starts when a job applicant hands in a job application (highest frequency start activity).

1. **Hand In Job Application** (source activity, always first).
   - Leads to Telephonic Screening or direct skip to next merge (frequencies show ~88% go to Telephonic Screening, 12% direct reject).

2. **Decision: Proceed to Initial Screening?**
   - Exclusive decision: Telephonic Screening (freq ~88%) or skip directly (leading to early rejection or Screening Interview path).
   - **Telephonic Screening** → optional Background Check or Screening Interview.
     - From Telephonic Screening: To Background Check (45%), Screening Interview (44%), or direct reject (11%).

3. **Flexible Screening Phase (parallel and optional):**
   - **Screening Interview** and **Background Check** can occur in either order or one skipped (due to tau splits/joins and skips in Petri net).
     - Common variants: Telephonic → Background → Screening or Telephonic → Screening → Background.
     - Sync after both or skips via tauJoin_5 → decision for Coding Interview, Extensive Background Check, or Reject.

4. **Decision: Proceed to Technical Checks/Interviews?** (Inclusive gateway allowing options/parallels).
   - **Background Check** → Coding Interview (16%), Extensive Background Check (12%), Reject (16%).
   - **Screening Interview** → Coding Interview (17%), Extensive Background Check (12%), Reject (16%).
   - **Extensive Background Check** optional after Background Check or Screening (10-12% usage), then to Coding or Reject.

5. **Technical Interview Phase (highly parallel and skippable, various orders via skips and tau splits):**
   - **Coding Interview** (gateway after screening).
   - Inclusive/parallel branches for **Frontend Interview**, **Backend Interview**, **ML Interview** (orders vary: e.g., Coding → Frontend → ML → Backend; loops between ML/Frontend/Backend).
     - Skips allow bypassing (e.g., skip Frontend to Backend).
     - Rejects possible after any technical interview (e.g., 4-8% after Coding/ML/Frontend/Backend).
   - Petri net shows tauSplit_14 splitting to loops/skips for these interviews, merging via tauJoin_10.

6. **Decision: Proceed to Final Stages?** (After technical sync).
   - To Behavioral Interview, Reject, or loops.

7. **Behavioral Interview Phase (with rework loops):**
   - **Behavioral Interview** → loops back to itself (freq 1032, short perf ~47ms, indicating repeats), or to Offer (21%), Reject (21%).
   - Can follow after any technical combo.

8. **Decision: Make Offer or Reject?** (Multiple end points).
   - **Make Job Offer** → loops to itself (freq 861, perf ~71ms), or Reject (rare, 110).
   - **Application Rejected** → can loop to more Behavioral (740), Offer (690), or itself (654), indicating post-reject rework/escalations.
   - Ends at sink after tauJoin_15 (success/reject merge).

**Parallel Activities and Synchronization Points:**
- Screening Interview and Background Check can parallel (but often sequential due to variants).
- Technical interviews (Coding, Frontend, Backend, ML) via tauSplit_9/14 allowing concurrent or skipped execution, sync at tauJoin_10.
