=== PETRI NET (ALPHA MINER) INTERPRETATION GUIDE ===

The Alpha Miner discovers Petri nets based on ordering relations in the event log.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
--------------
The output follows this structure:
"If I have a Petri net:

places: [ place1, place2, ... ]
transitions: [ (id, 'Label'), ... ]
arcs: [ source->target, ... ]

initial marking: ['source:1']
final marking: ['sink:1']"

KEY DIFFERENCES FROM INDUCTIVE MINER:
- Places are often named as tuples like ({'A'}, {'B'}) representing the activities they connect
- No silent/tau transitions (Alpha miner doesn't handle invisible transitions)
- May produce disconnected or incomplete nets for complex logs

ORDERING RELATIONS USED:
1. Direct succession (>): A directly followed by B in some trace
2. Causality (->): A > B and not B > A
3. Parallelism (||): A > B and B > A (both orders observed)
4. Choice (#): A and B never directly follow each other

READING THE OUTPUT:
- Places named as {A}, {B} tuples: Connect activity A to activity B
- 'start' and 'end' places: Process entry/exit points
- All transitions are visible (labeled activities)
- Arcs show flow: Activity->place or place->Activity

LIMITATIONS:
- Cannot handle loops of length 1 or 2
- Sensitive to noise
- Assumes complete event logs
- May miss implicit dependencies

WHEN TO USE:
- Simple, well-structured processes
- Educational purposes
- Quick initial discovery
- Logs with clear sequential patterns
