IDEAL Process Model

Process overview and purpose
- Purpose: A controlled, efficient hiring workflow that screens applicants, conducts appropriate level checks, evaluates technical and behavioral fit, and ends with a governed decision (offer or rejection).
- Design principles applied:
  - Early, well-defined decision gates.
  - Role-aligned interview selection (avoid unnecessary tracks).
  - Conditional, risk-based background checks.
  - Parallelization where safe (to reduce lead time), with clear synchronization before decision.
  - Clear, single termination gateway to Offer or Rejection.

Key stakeholders and roles involved
- Roles are not specified in the source; activities are defined without lane assignments.

Start conditions and triggers
- Trigger: Candidate submits application (start event).
- Precondition: A requisition exists and is open for hiring (implicit business context).

Detailed step-by-step process flow
1) Hand In Job Application (Start)
   - Capture applicant data and desired role/track.

2) Telephonic Screening
   - Purpose: Verify basic qualifications, communication, and role alignment.
   - Decision (exclusive):
     - Fail: Route to Application Rejected (End).
     - Pass: Proceed to next steps.

3) Screening Interview and Standard Background Check (parallel)
   - Parallel split:
     - Screening Interview: Deeper initial assessment.
     - Background Check (standard scope): Identity/employment/education verification at a baseline level.
   - Rationale: Parallelization shortens cycle time without compromising decision quality.

4) Conditional Extensive Background Check (inclusive/conditional)
   - Decision based on risk triggers from standard check or screening outcomes (e.g., discrepancies):
     - If triggered: Perform Extensive Background Check (after standard check completes).
     - If not triggered: Skip this step.
   - Synchronization point:
     - Proceed to final decision only after all required checks (standard and, if applicable, extensive) are complete.
   - Note: Coding Interview scheduling can proceed in parallel, but offer decision must await completion of required checks.

5) Coding Interview
   - Conduct standardized coding assessment aligned to the target role.

6) Technical track selection (exclusive gateway)
   - Choose exactly one role-appropriate track based on requisition:
     - Backend Interview
     - Frontend Interview
     - ML Interview
   - Optionally, allow more than one track only when the requisition expressly spans multiple tracks; otherwise default to a single selected track to avoid over-processing.

7) Behavioral Interview
   - Conduct behavioral/cultural fit interview after technical assessment.
   - This step was present but unordered in the AS-IS; in the IDEAL model, it is placed explicitly after technical evaluation to standardize sequencing.

8) Consolidated evaluation and decision (single decision gateway)
   - Inputs required:
     - Results of Coding Interview
     - Results of selected technical track
     - Behavioral Interview outcome
     - Background Check results (standard, and extensive if triggered)
   - Decision outcomes (exclusive):
     - Make Job Offer (End): If all required evaluations meet defined thresholds and background checks are clear.
     - Application Rejected (End): If thresholds are not met or checks fail.

Parallel activities and synchronization points
- Parallel:

    - Selected technical track
    - Behavioral Interview

Exception handling and error paths

- Background check discrepancies:

  - If material: route to Application Rejected.
- Technical interview inconclusive:
  - Allow a single re-interview in the same track; if still inconclusive or below threshold, reject.

Rework loops and escalation procedures

- Escalation:

End conditions and outcomes
