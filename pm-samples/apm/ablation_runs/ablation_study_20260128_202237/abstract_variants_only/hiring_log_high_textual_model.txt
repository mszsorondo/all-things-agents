IDEAL Process Model

Process overview and purpose
- This model defines a cleaned, governed hiring process from application to a single, final outcome (rejection or offer), removing rework, duplicate decision logging, and allowing only well-controlled, necessary paths.

Key stakeholders and roles involved
- The source does not specify roles. Role-to-task assignments are therefore omitted. The model assumes activities are executed by appropriate parties without adding new actors.

Detailed step-by-step process flow

Start conditions and triggers
- Start event: Hand In Job Application.
- Trigger: A submitted application initiates the evaluation pipeline.

Standardized activities and sequence
1) Hand In Job Application (start)

2) Telephonic Screening (mandatory first screen)
   - Decision:
     a) Not suitable -> Application Rejected (terminal).
     b) Suitable -> proceed.

3) Screening Interview (standard face-to-face/virtual screen)
   - Decision:
     a) Not suitable -> Application Rejected (terminal).
     b) Suitable -> proceed.

4) Background Check (standard scope)
   - Decision:
     a) Clear/no concerns -> proceed.
     b) Concerns present -> Extensive Background Check (XBC).

5) Extensive Background Check (conditional; only if required)
   - Decision:
     a) Concerns confirmed -> Application Rejected (terminal).
     b) Concerns resolved -> proceed.

6) Technical Interview stage (configured set; each used at most once)
   - Inclusive selection of relevant interviews based on the candidate’s assessed profile (only from the existing set; no new activities introduced):
     - Coding Interview (if applicable)
     - ML Interview (if applicable)
     - Backend Interview (if applicable)
     - Frontend Interview (if applicable)
   - Execute selected interviews in a planned sequence (no concurrency required). Each interview occurs at most once.

7) Behavioral Interview (exactly once)
   - Occurs after the selected technical interviews are complete.

8) Final decision (single, authoritative)
   - Evaluate all collected results.
   - Outcomes (exactly one, terminal):
     a) Application Rejected (terminal; no further activities allowed).
     b) Make Job Offer (terminal; no further activities allowed).

Decision points with criteria and outcomes
- After Telephonic Screening: suitability for further evaluation -> proceed or reject.
- After Screening Interview: confirm fit before background -> proceed or reject.
- After Background Check: if concerns, require XBC; else proceed to interviews.

- After all planned interviews: single final decision -> reject or make offer.

Parallel activities and synchronization points
- No parallelism required. The process relies on clear, sequential stage gates.
- Synchronization points:

- Data governance:
