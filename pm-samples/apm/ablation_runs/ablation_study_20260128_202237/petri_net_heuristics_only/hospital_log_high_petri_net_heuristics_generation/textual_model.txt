AS-IS Process Model

Process overview and purpose
This process covers a patient episode from initial registration, through clinical examinations, diagnosis, treatment, assessment of treatment outcome, and discharge. The description reflects the behavior discovered by a Heuristics Miner Petri net, including optional steps and a rework loop upon unsuccessful treatment outcomes.

  - Examination (standard)
  - Expert Examination (specialized)
- Decision logic: The model does not encode explicit decision criteria; behavior shows that only one of these is taken per case. The choice is recorded and later combined with the registration source by implicit (hidden) transitions to enable downstream steps.

- Thorough Examination is an optional step that may precede Diagnosis.
- Synchronization/enablement: Hidden joins combine two facts before allowing Thorough Examination:
  - The registration source (ER or FD), and
  - The type of initial examination completed (Examination or Expert Examination).
- Control logic: If conditions indicate the need, Thorough Examination occurs; otherwise the process can proceed toward Diagnosis without this step.

5) Diagnosis (after required preconditions)
- Diagnosis is performed once the model’s hidden joins establish that at least one prior examination (Examination or Expert Examination) has been completed. If Thorough Examination occurred, that also enables Diagnosis.
- Outcome: Upon completion of Diagnosis, the process moves to Treatment.

6) Treatment
- A single Treatment activity follows Diagnosis.

7) Treatment outcome and branching
- Decision point (exclusive):
  - Treatment unsuccessful: The process returns to Diagnosis (rework loop). This allows iterative cycles of re-diagnosis and re-treatment until a successful outcome is achieved. There is no limit encoded on the number of cycles.
  - Treatment successful: The model enforces two conditions before discharge:
    - A confirmation step recorded as Treatment successful.
    - A readiness token stemming from completion of Treatment.
  - Synchronization: Discharge requires both the completion of Treatment successful and the post-Treatment readiness, i.e., Discharge will not occur until the success confirmation is recorded.

8) End conditions and outcomes
- Discharge: Once Treatment successful is recorded and the discharge readiness from Treatment is present, Discharge completes the episode and ends the process.

Decision points with criteria and possible outcomes (summary)
