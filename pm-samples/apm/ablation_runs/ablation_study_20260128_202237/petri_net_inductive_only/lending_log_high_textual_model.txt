Process overview and purpose
- Purpose: Define a controlled, compliant loan intake and decisioning flow using only activities present in the source but enforcing sound sequencing and governance.
- Goals:

  - Remove contradictory and unsafe bypasses (no skipping of underwriter submission where required; no skipping signing upon approval).
  - Keep useful concurrency (co-signer handling and collateral assessment) with clear synchronization and bounded rework.

Key stakeholders and roles involved
- Roles remain implied (not explicitly specified in the source):
  - Borrower: signs agreement; provides information.
  - Front-office/loan officer: handles appointment, application intake, collateral visit (as implied).
