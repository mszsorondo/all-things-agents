# LOAN APPLICATION AND APPROVAL PROCESS - AS-IS STATE

## 1. PROCESS OVERVIEW AND PURPOSE

This process describes the current state of a loan application and approval system as observed through process mining analysis of 10,000 cases. The process begins when a borrower requests an appointment with a lending institution and progresses through credit verification, underwriting, and potentially loan approval or denial. The process exhibits significant variability in execution paths, extensive rework loops, premature denials, and inconsistent examination procedures that lead to inefficiencies and extended cycle times.

**Primary Purpose:** To evaluate loan applications from initial contact through final approval or rejection, including borrower verification, collateral assessment, co-signer arrangements, and underwriting review.

**Business Context:** This is a financial services credit origination process handling consumer or commercial loan applications with varying risk profiles and complexity levels.

## 2. KEY STAKEHOLDERS AND ROLES

Based on the activities identified in the process, the following roles are involved:

- **Appointment Coordinator/Scheduler:** Manages appointment requests and scheduling
- **Loan Officer/Application Processor:** Receives credit applications and performs initial borrower verification
- **Credit Analyst/Verifier:** Conducts detailed verification of borrower information and creditworthiness
- **Collateral Assessment Specialist:** Conducts physical visits to assess collateral value and condition
- **Risk Manager/Loan Officer:** Determines need for co-signers based on risk assessment
- **Underwriter:** Makes final credit decisions and loan approval/denial determinations
- **Loan Closing Officer:** Manages loan agreement signing for approved applications
- **System/Automated Process:** Handles certain examination skipping logic and routing

## 5. KEY CHALLENGES AND PAIN POINTS (AS-IS STATE)

### 5.1 Process Design Issues

**1. Premature Appointment Denials**
- **Problem:** 9.62% of applicants denied before submitting application
- **Impact:** Poor customer experience; lost revenue; no documented rationale
- **Root cause:** Likely arbitrary or overly restrictive pre-screening without proper criteria

**2. High Post-Processing Denial Rate**
- **Problem:** 73.73% overall rejection/denial rate, with 47.87% denied after full underwriting
- **Impact:** Massive resource waste on applications that should be screened earlier
- **Root cause:** Weak front-end qualification; misaligned expectations; poor credit policy communication

**3. Examination Bypass (Compliance Risk)**
- **Problem:** 332 cases (3.67%) skip underwriter review and proceed directly to signing
- **Impact:** HIGH COMPLIANCE RISK; potential for inappropriate credit approvals; regulatory violation risk
- **Root cause:** System loophole; pressure to approve quickly; inadequate controls

**4. Excessive Collateral Assessment Rework**
- **Problem:** 50-60% of collateral assessments require multiple visits (up to 7 observed)
- **Impact:** Severe cycle time inflation; resource waste; customer frustration; delayed decisions
- **Root cause:** Unclear assessment standards; inadequate initial inspection; poor training; changing requirements

**5. Unclear Enhancement Requirement Logic**
- **Problem:** Inconsistent application of co-signer and collateral requirements; bidirectional flows suggest indecision
- **Impact:** Unpredictable process paths; extended cycle times; confusion for applicants
- **Root cause:** Lack of clear risk-based decisioning rules; manual judgment without guidelines
