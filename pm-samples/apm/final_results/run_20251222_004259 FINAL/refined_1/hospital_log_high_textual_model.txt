# AS-IS Process Model

Process overview and scope
- Purpose: End-to-end patient episode from first registration through assessment, diagnosis, treatment (with possible rework), and discharge, as observed in event logs.
- Scope: One case per patient episode. The process is strictly sequential (no concurrency), with exclusive choices and a single rework loop around Treatment/Diagnosis.

Activities
- Register at FD, Register at ER (mutually exclusive case starts)
- Examination, Expert Examination (mutually exclusive preliminary assessments)
- Thorough Examination (optional deep assessment)
- Diagnosis
- Treatment
- Treatment successful, Treatment unsuccessful
- Discharge

Start and end
- Start: Exactly one of Register at FD or Register at ER occurs first and exactly once per case.
- End: Discharge occurs exactly once and only as the final event in the case.

Pre-diagnosis assessment segment (occurs once per case, before the first Diagnosis)
- Immediately after registration, exactly one of the following mutually exclusive routes is taken:
  1) Examination → [optional Thorough Examination] → Diagnosis
  2) Expert Examination → [optional Thorough Examination] → Diagnosis
  3) Skip preliminary exam → [optional Thorough Examination] → Diagnosis
- Constraints:
  - At most one of Examination or Expert Examination may occur (they are mutually exclusive and occur at most once).
  - Thorough Examination may occur at most once, only in this pre-diagnosis segment. It may follow either Examination, Expert Examination, or a direct skip from registration.
  - No Examination, Expert Examination, or Thorough Examination is allowed after any Diagnosis.

Diagnosis and treatment cycle
- The first Diagnosis immediately follows the pre-diagnosis segment.
- After any Diagnosis, exactly one Treatment occurs.
- After each Treatment, exactly one of the following mutually exclusive outcomes occurs:
  1) Treatment successful → Discharge (case ends).
  2) Treatment unsuccessful → return to Diagnosis (rework loop). The cycle Diagnosis → Treatment may repeat multiple times due to repeated unsuccessful treatments.
  3) Direct Discharge without a recorded success label (Treatment → Discharge) (case ends).
- Constraints:
  - Every Treatment must be directly preceded by exactly one Diagnosis.
  - Treatment unsuccessful must be followed by Diagnosis (not Discharge), before any subsequent Treatment.
  - Treatment successful may occur at most once per case and only immediately after Treatment, and must be followed by Discharge.
  - When the “no-outcome-recorded” path is taken, Treatment goes directly to Discharge; no Treatment successful occurs in that cycle.
  - No activities occur after Discharge.

Allowed high-level ordering (grammar-like view)
- Start:
  - (Register at FD | Register at ER)
- Pre-diagnosis segment (exactly one of):
  - Examination [→ Thorough Examination]? → Diagnosis
  - Expert Examination [→ Thorough Examination]? → Diagnosis
  - [Skip exam] [→ Thorough Examination]? → Diagnosis
- Treatment loop:
  - Repeat 1..N times:
    - Diagnosis → Treatment → (Treatment unsuccessful → continue loop | Treatment successful → Discharge end | Discharge end)

Explicit validity notes and constraints (to ensure precision)
- Valid:
  - Registration → Examination → Diagnosis → Treatment → Treatment unsuccessful → Diagnosis → Treatment → Treatment successful → Discharge
  - Registration → Expert Examination → Thorough Examination → Diagnosis → Treatment → Discharge (no success label)
  - Registration → [skip exams] → Diagnosis → Treatment → Treatment successful → Discharge
  - Multi-loop variants with repeated Treatment unsuccessful (e.g., two or three loops) before final success or direct discharge.
- Invalid (reject):
  - Any Examination/Expert Examination/Thorough Examination after any Diagnosis.
  - Both Examination and Expert Examination in the same case.
  - More than one occurrence of Examination, Expert Examination, or Thorough Examination.
  - Treatment before any Diagnosis.
  - Treatment successful or Treatment unsuccessful without an immediately preceding Treatment.
  - Treatment unsuccessful → Discharge (disallowed).
  - Any activity after Discharge.
  - Multiple Discharges or multiple Registrations in one case.

Notes on observed data behavior (from mining)
- The “Treatment → Discharge” path without recording “Treatment successful” is allowed (observed) and ends the case.
- Rework via Treatment unsuccessful may repeat multiple times before eventual success or direct discharge.
- No concurrency is present; all paths are strictly sequential with XOR branching and a single rework loop around Diagnosis/Treatment.
