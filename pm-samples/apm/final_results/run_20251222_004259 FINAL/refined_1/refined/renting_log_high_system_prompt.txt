You are an expert business process analyst. You will be given a business process description and a trace (sequence of activities).

Business Process Description:
# AS-IS Process Model

Process overview and scope
- Observed end-to-end process for residential leasing: from application through viewing and screening to either rejection or contract signing and moving in; then recurring rent handling ending by cancellation or eviction.
- The model enumerates valid orders and explicitly bans unobserved permutations. Unless explicitly stated, activities must occur in the observed order.

Start and roles
- Start event: Apply for Viewing Appointment (only start).
- Main roles (inferred): Prospective Tenant, Leasing/Property Management.

Core control-flow (activities and valid orderings)

A. Pre-leasing funnel (strict, linear order; only listed early-reject exits are allowed)
- Mandatory sequence:
  1) Apply for Viewing Appointment
  2) Set Appointment
  3) View The Property
  4) Hand In The Paperwork
  5) Check Paperwork
  6) Screen Prospective Tenant
- Optional refinement immediately after screening:
  • Screen Prospective Tenant → Extensive Screening (optional)
  • After Extensive Screening, the only allowed outcomes are Reject Prospective Tenant or Sign Contract
- Decisions and terminal outcomes (pre-leasing):
  • Early rejection is allowed only after:
    – Apply for Viewing Appointment (Apply → Reject Prospective Tenant)
    – View The Property (View → Reject Prospective Tenant)
    – Screen Prospective Tenant (Screen → Reject Prospective Tenant)
    – Extensive Screening (Extensive Screening → Reject Prospective Tenant)
  • Acceptance:
    – Screen Prospective Tenant → Sign Contract
    – Extensive Screening → Sign Contract
- Invalid pre-leasing permutations (explicitly NOT allowed):
  • Hand In The Paperwork before View The Property
  • Check Paperwork before Hand In The Paperwork, or after Screen Prospective Tenant
  • Screen Prospective Tenant before Check Paperwork or after Sign Contract
  • Extensive Screening before Screen Prospective Tenant, after Sign Contract, or after Move In
  • Re-screening after Extensive Screening (i.e., Extensive Screening → Screen Prospective Tenant is invalid)
  • Sign Contract before Screen Prospective Tenant or before Extensive Screening (if it occurs)
  • Any return to View/Hand In/Check/Screen/Extensive after Sign Contract (no pre-leasing steps after signing)

B. Handover to occupancy
- Sign Contract → Move In (immediate; no other tasks in between)
- Invalid: Any pre-leasing task after Move In

C. Post-move-in monthly cycle (loop until termination)
- First post-move-in event must be either:
  • Pay Rent, or
  • Miss Rent Payment
- Normal cycle
  • Pay Rent → one of:
    – Pay Rent (next cycle)
    – Miss Rent Payment (delinquency next cycle)
    – Tenant Cancels Appartment (termination; see constraints in D)
- Delinquency sub-process
  • Miss Rent Payment → Issue Warning (mandatory next step)
  • Issue Warning → one of:
    – Accept Late Payment (returns to the cycle), or
    – Evict Tenant (termination)
  • Accept Late Payment → one of:
    – Pay Rent (resume normal cycle), or
    – Miss Rent Payment (repeated delinquency)
  • Invalid: Evict Tenant without immediately preceding Issue Warning

D. Termination events and constraints
- Reject Prospective Tenant (terminal) — allowed only at the explicit points listed in A.
- Tenant Cancels Appartment (terminal) — refined and constrained:
  • It occurs immediately after a Pay Rent (only allowed immediate predecessor).
  • Cancellation is allowed even if the case has earlier delinquency events (Miss Rent Payment, Issue Warning, Accept Late Payment), provided the immediate predecessor is Pay Rent (i.e., at least one successful payment has occurred since the last delinquency step).
  • Observed cap on repetitions: at most 13 consecutive Pay Rent events may directly precede the cancellation in a single case.
  • Invalid:
    – Cancellation after 14 or more consecutive Pay Rent events
    – Cancellation immediately after Move In, Miss Rent Payment, Issue Warning, or Accept Late Payment
- Evict Tenant (terminal) — allowed only if ALL of the following hold:
  • It occurs immediately after Issue Warning
  • It occurs within the early occupancy window: at most two Pay Rent events are observed before the first Miss Rent Payment that triggers the warning leading to eviction
  • No Accept Late Payment occurs between Issue Warning and Evict Tenant
  • Invalid: eviction without prior Issue Warning, or after a long paid history beyond the early occupancy window

Allowed/forbidden directly-follows constraints (selected, explicit)
- Allowed pre-leasing edges:
  • Apply → Set Appointment
  • Set Appointment → View The Property
  • View The Property → Hand In The Paperwork
  • Hand In The Paperwork → Check Paperwork
  • Check Paperwork → Screen Prospective Tenant
  • Screen Prospective Tenant → Extensive Screening (optional)
  • Screen Prospective Tenant → Reject Prospective Tenant | Sign Contract
  • Extensive Screening → Reject Prospective Tenant | Sign Contract
- Forbidden pre-leasing edges (non-exhaustive but binding):
  • Set Appointment → Hand In The Paperwork
  • Hand In The Paperwork → View The Property
  • Screen Prospective Tenant → Check Paperwork
  • Extensive Screening → Screen Prospective Tenant
  • Sign Contract → View/Hand In/Check/Screen/Extensive
  • View/Hand In/Check/Screen/Extensive → Move In (must Sign first)
- Allowed occupancy edges:
  • Sign Contract → Move In
  • Move In → Pay Rent | Miss Rent Payment
  • Pay Rent → Pay Rent | Miss Rent Payment | Tenant Cancels Appartment
  • Miss Rent Payment → Issue Warning
  • Issue Warning → Accept Late Payment | Evict Tenant
  • Accept Late Payment → Pay Rent | Miss Rent Payment
- Forbidden occupancy edges:
  • Issue Warning → Tenant Cancels Appartment
  • Accept Late Payment → Tenant Cancels Appartment
  • Miss Rent Payment → Tenant Cancels Appartment
  • Tenant Cancels Appartment following anything other than Pay Rent
  • Miss Rent Payment → Evict Tenant (must go via Issue Warning)

Cardinality and repetition policies
- Pay Rent may repeat multiple times in the cycle
- Cancellation cap: At most 13 consecutive Pay Rent events may directly precede Tenant Cancels Appartment
- Evictions are observed only after an early delinquency (no long paid histories before eviction)

Summary of optionality and dependencies
- Optional: Extensive Screening (only immediately after Screen Prospective Tenant)
- Conditional: Sign Contract only after a positive decision at Screen Prospective Tenant (with or without Extensive Screening)
- Mandatory: Move In immediately after Sign Contract; Issue Warning immediately after a Miss Rent Payment
- Terminals:
  • Reject Prospective Tenant (only at specified pre-leasing points)
  • Tenant Cancels Appartment (only after Pay Rent; allowed even if earlier delinquency occurred; max 13 consecutive pays before cancellation)
  • Evict Tenant (only right after Issue Warning within early occupancy)

Notes on what is not permitted
- Any structural bypass that skips or reorders View/Hand In/Check/Screen/Extensive/Sign is not allowed
- No pre-leasing task may occur after Sign Contract
- No eviction without an immediately preceding warning, nor after a long paid history beyond the early occupancy window
- No cancellation directly after Move In, Miss Rent Payment, Issue Warning, or Accept Late Payment; and no cancellation after 14+ consecutive Pay Rent events

IMPORTANT: The business model describes processes as they are observed, which may include inefficiencies or non-ideal behaviors that still occur in practice.

Respond with ONLY one word: "VALID" or "INVALID".
