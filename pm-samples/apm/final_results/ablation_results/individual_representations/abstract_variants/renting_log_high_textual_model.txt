IDEAL Process Model

Process overview and purpose
- Purpose: Efficiently convert qualified interest into signed leases while minimizing wasted activity and managing tenancy with clear, enforceable late-payment handling and controlled termination paths.
- Design intent: Move qualification earlier, constrain repetitive late-payment loops, and standardize decisions while preserving all observed legitimate end states.

Key stakeholders and roles involved
- Prospective Tenant/Tenant.
- Internal property management/letting function (responsible for appointment scheduling, document checks, screening, contracting, and nonpayment handling).
- Note: Specific internal role titles are not provided; responsibilities align to the activity names observed.

Detailed step-by-step process flow (to-be)

Start conditions and triggers
- Trigger: Apply for Viewing Appointment.

1) Apply for Viewing Appointment -> Hand In The Paperwork -> Check Paperwork

     a) Paperwork complete and acceptable -> proceed to screening.
     b) Paperwork incomplete/unacceptable -> Reject Prospective Tenant (end).
       - Note: This uses the existing rejection outcome; the trigger is better-placed validation.

   - Standard screening performed before any viewing.
   - Decision:
     a) Pass -> proceed to appointment setting.
     b) Borderline/risk flags -> Extensive Screening (existing deeper vetting activity).
        - Outcome of Extensive Screening:
          i) Pass -> proceed to appointment setting.
          ii) Fail -> Reject Prospective Tenant (end).
     c) Fail -> Reject Prospective Tenant (end).

3) Set Appointment -> View The Property
   - Only qualified applicants are scheduled for viewing.
   - Decision post-view:
     a) Applicant declines or withdraws interest -> Reject Prospective Tenant (end).
