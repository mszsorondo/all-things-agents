Business Process Description:
# AS-IS Process Model

## Process Overview and Purpose
The AS-IS process represents a loan application and approval workflow for a financial institution. This process captures the current state of loan processing, including all observed variations, rework loops, and compliance issues discovered through process mining analysis. The process begins when a customer requests an appointment and ends with either loan approval, denial, or application rejection.

## Key Stakeholders and Roles Involved
* **Customers/Applicants**: Individuals seeking loan approval
* **Front Desk/Appointment Schedulers**: Handle initial appointment requests
* **Loan Officers/Application Processors**: Collect and verify borrower information
* **Underwriters**: Evaluate loan applications and make approval decisions
* **Collateral Assessors**: Conduct site visits to evaluate collateral
* **Credit Department**: Handles co-signer requests and credit verification

## Detailed Step-by-Step Process Flow

### 1. Start Conditions and Triggers
* **Trigger**: Customer initiates loan application process by requesting an appointment
* **Entry Point**: "Request Appointment" activity
* **Initial Documentation**: Customer provides basic contact information and loan inquiry details

### 2. Appointment Management Phase
* **Activity 1: Request Appointment**
  * Customer submits appointment request through various channels (phone, online, in-person)
  * Basic customer information collected
  * **Decision Point**: Appointment availability and eligibility check
    * **Outcome A (10.6% of cases)**: Appointment Denied → Process terminates immediately
    * **Outcome B (89.4% of cases)**: Proceed to Set Appointment

* **Activity 2: Set Appointment**
  * Schedule specific date and time for application submission
  * Confirm appointment details with customer
  * Provide pre-application checklist

### 3. Application Submission Phase
* **Activity 3: Hand In Credit Application**
  * Customer submits completed loan application form
  * Required documentation collected (ID, income proof, etc.)
  * Application date stamped and assigned tracking number

### 4. Information Verification Phase
* **Activity 4: Verify Borrowers Information**
  * Loan officer reviews application completeness
  * Initial credit check performed
  * Documentation validation
  * **Critical Decision Point**: Multiple paths emerge based on verification results
    * **Path A (51.1% of cases)**: Proceed directly to Submit File to Underwriter
    * **Path B (18.0% of cases)**: Application Rejected → Process terminates
    * **Path C (13.6% of cases)**: Request Co-Signer On Loan
    * **Path D (13.6% of cases)**: Make Visit to Assess Collateral
    * **Path E (3.7% of cases)**: skipped_examination → Proceed to Sign Loan Agreement

### 5. Additional Requirements Phase (Parallel/Alternative Paths)

#### **Co-Signer Request Path**
* **Activity: Request Co-Signer On Loan**
  * Additional credit support required
  * Co-signer application forms generated
  * Co-signer credit check initiated
  * **Subsequent Options**:
    * Proceed to Submit File to Underwriter (46.5% of co-signer requests)
    * Make Visit to Assess Collateral (33.0% of co-signer requests)
    * Combination of both paths (20.5% of co-signer requests)

#### **Collateral Assessment Path**
* **Activity: Make Visit to Assess Collateral**
  * Site visit scheduled to evaluate property/assets
  * Physical inspection conducted
  * Collateral valuation report prepared
  * **Critical Issue**: Significant rework loops observed
    * Multiple consecutive collateral assessments (up to 8 repetitions observed)
    * 44.3% of collateral visits lead to additional visits
    * 42.4% of collateral visits lead to co-signer requests
    * Only 13.3% proceed directly to underwriting

#### **Examination Skipping Path**
* **Activity: skipped_examination**
  * Bypass of normal verification procedures
  * Direct progression to loan signing
  * Occurs in 3.7% of cases with questionable compliance

### 6. Underwriting and Decision Phase
* **Activity: Submit File to Underwriter**
  * Complete application package assembled
  * Risk assessment documentation prepared
  * File submitted to underwriting department
  * **Performance Issue**: Significant bottleneck with average processing time of 48-82 hours

* **Underwriting Decision**:
  * **Outcome A (67.7% of underwritten cases)**: Loan Denied
  * **Outcome B (32.3% of underwritten cases)**: Sign Loan Agreement

### 7. Finalization Phase
* **Activity: Sign Loan Agreement**
  * Loan terms and conditions finalized
  * Legal documentation prepared
  * Customer signs loan agreement
  * Funds disbursement scheduled

## Process End States
1. **Appointment Denied** (10.6% of cases): Process terminates at initial stage
2. **Application Rejected** (18.0% of cases): Process terminates after verification
3. **Loan Denied** (47.5% of underwritten cases): Process terminates after underwriting
4. **Sign Loan Agreement** (22.6% of total cases): Successful loan approval

## Process Metrics and Performance Characteristics
* **Total Cases Analyzed**: 9,038 loan applications
* **Average Process Duration**: Highly variable based on path
  * Direct approval path: ~339,454 seconds (94.3 hours)
  * Denial paths: ~337,687 seconds (93.8 hours)
  * Complex paths with rework: Up to 469,827 seconds (130.5 hours)
* **Key Performance Issues**:
  * High denial rate (76.1% of cases end in denial/rejection)
  * Excessive rework in collateral assessment
  * Inconsistent verification procedures
  * Long underwriting delays

## Key Challenges and Pain Points
1. **Excessive Rework Loops**: Collateral assessment shows significant repetition, with some cases requiring up to 8 consecutive visits
2. **Inconsistent Verification**: "skipped_examination" path bypasses standard procedures, creating compliance risks
3. **High Denial Rates**: 76.1% of applications don't progress to approval, indicating inefficient screening
4. **Process Variability**: 32 distinct process variants identified, suggesting lack of standardization
5. **Bottlenecks**: Underwriting phase shows longest processing times
6. **Resource Waste**: Multiple collateral assessments without clear decision criteria
7. **Compliance Gaps**: Skipped examinations create regulatory exposure
8. **Customer Experience**: Long processing times and high denial rates negatively impact customer satisfaction

## Process Compliance and Quality Issues
* **Regulatory Risk**: Skipped examinations violate standard lending protocols
* **Quality Control**: Inconsistent application of verification criteria
* **Documentation**: Lack of standardized decision documentation
* **Audit Trail**: Complex rework loops make process auditing difficult

## Resource Utilization Patterns
* **Underutilization**: Appointment denial at initial stage wastes scheduling resources
* **Overutilization**: Collateral assessment teams burdened with repetitive visits
* **Inefficient Routing**: Multiple handoffs between verification, co-signer requests, and collateral assessment create coordination challenges
