### IDEAL Process Model

#### Process Overview and Purpose
The IDEAL process model is an improved, streamlined version of the hospital ER/FD patient treatment process. It enforces a standardized, safe, and efficient flow by mandating essential examinations, limiting rework to a maximum of two treatment attempts with escalation, eliminating unsafe shortcuts like direct discharge without verification, and rationalizing exam paths into a sequential triage. The purpose remains patient registration, accurate diagnosis, effective treatment, and safe discharge, but with reduced variability, higher compliance, and better outcomes under good governance.

#### Key Stakeholders and Roles Involved
Roles are not explicitly specified in the source models. Activities imply involvement of reception staff (registration), triage nurses/doctors (examinations), physicians (diagnosis, treatment), and discharge coordinators.

#### Detailed Step-by-Step Process Flow
1. **Start Conditions and Triggers**: Patient arrival at ER or FD, with immediate registration.

2. **Registration (Exclusive Gateway)**:
   - XOR: "Register at ER" or "Register at FD" (based on location).
   - Leads to triage.

3. **Standardized Triage/Examination Sequence (Sequential with Optional Expert)**:
   - Mandatory "Examination" (combines common paths).
   - Followed by decision: If high severity → "Expert Examination"; else → proceed.
   - Then mandatory "Thorough Examination".
   - No skips; ensures accurate initial assessment (removes observed shortcuts).

4. **Diagnosis**:
   - Single "Diagnosis" activity post-exams.

5. **Treatment with Limited Rework Loop**:
   - "Treatment".
   - Decision post-treatment:
     - If "Treatment successful" → proceed to discharge.
     - If "Treatment unsuccessful" → Loop back to Diagnosis (max 2 times).
     - After 2 failures → Escalate to senior specialist (new merge point, implied synchronization).

6. **Discharge**:
   - Mandatory "Discharge" only after success verification.
   - No direct Treatment→Discharge.

7. **Decision Points**:
   - Registration: Location-based (ER vs. FD).
   - Post-Examination: Severity check (yes → Expert Exam; no → Thorough Exam).
   - Post-Treatment: Outcome (success → discharge; unsuccessful → loop/escalate).
   - Loop limit: Counter-based (≤2 → loop; >2 → escalate).
   - Outcomes: Proceed, optional expert, limited retry, escalate, or end.

8. **Parallel Activities and Synchronization**: None required; fully sequential for simplicity and safety. Synchronization at merges (e.g., post-exams to Diagnosis).

9. **Exception Handling and Error Paths**:
   - Unsuccessful treatment: Limited retries.
   - Max loops exceeded: Escalate to "Expert Treatment Review" (inferred from expert exams, ensures resolution).

10. **Rework Loops and Escalation Procedures**:
    - Controlled loop: Max 2x Treatment unsuccessful → Diagnosis.
    - Escalation after 2 failures: Route to senior review before retry or alternative discharge.

11. **End Conditions and Outcomes**:
    - Successful discharge after verification.
    - Escalated resolution discharge.
    - No premature ends.

#### Process Metrics and Performance Characteristics
- Expected straight-through time: ~240k-260k units (reduced from avg. 320k by eliminating skips/loops).
- Loop cases: Limited to +120k per loop (max +240k).
- Target: 90%+ straight paths, <5% escalations.
- Bottlenecks resolved: Fixed exam sequence reduces variability; loop limits cut tail times.
- Compliance: 100% exam coverage, 100% success verification.

#### Key Improvements Made
- **Reduced Variability**: Single exam sequence with one optional branch (vs. inclusive skips).
- **Eliminated Risks**: No direct Discharge post-Treatment; mandatory success check.
- **Controlled Rework**: Max 2 loops + escalation (vs. unlimited 3+ loops).
- **Streamlined Flow**: Removed silent skips; enforced sequencing (exams → Diagnosis → Treatment).
- **Efficiency Gains**: Shorter avg. time (target 260k), fewer bottlenecks at Treatment.
- **Safety/Compliance**: Full triage mandatory, verified discharges; handles 99% variants conservatively.
- **Scalability**: Predictable flow supports higher volumes without proportional delays.
