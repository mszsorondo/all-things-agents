IDEAL PROCESS MODEL: RECRUITMENT AND HIRING PROCESS

================================================================================
1. PROCESS OVERVIEW AND PURPOSE
================================================================================

The IDEAL recruitment and hiring process represents a streamlined, efficient, and compliant approach to candidate evaluation and selection. This redesigned process maintains necessary quality gates while eliminating wasteful rework, establishing clear decision authority, and significantly reducing cycle time.

The IDEAL process transforms the chaotic AS-IS state into a structured, sequential evaluation framework with:
• Standardized activity sequencing
• Early and effective candidate filtering
• Clear decision criteria at each gate
• Elimination of circular dependencies and rework loops
• Streamlined technical interview approach
• Strong process governance and auditability
• Improved candidate experience
• Reduced time-to-hire while maintaining quality

Primary Purpose:
- Efficiently evaluate candidate qualifications against position requirements
- Conduct necessary verification and screening activities in optimal sequence
- Assess technical competencies through focused, non-redundant interviews
- Make timely, high-quality hiring decisions
- Deliver superior candidate experience throughout the process

Target Performance:
- Reduce average time-to-hire from 530,000 to <200,000 time units (60%+ reduction)
- Eliminate rework loops and circular interview patterns
- Increase early-stage rejection rate to filter unqualified candidates sooner
- Achieve 95%+ process compliance with standardized sequence
- Reduce average technical interviews per candidate from 3.8 to 1.5
- Eliminate terminal state violations (no rejection reversals without documented appeals)

================================================================================
2. KEY STAKEHOLDERS AND ROLES
================================================================================

The IDEAL process clearly defines roles and responsibilities:

CANDIDATE
• Submits application through standardized channels
• Participates in scheduled screening and interview activities
• Provides required documentation for verification
• Responds to communications within defined timeframes

HR RECRUITMENT TEAM (Process Owner)
• Manages end-to-end process coordination and candidate communication
• Conducts initial resume screening and telephonic screening
• Schedules all interviews and coordinates across stakeholders
• Ensures process compliance and timeline adherence
• Owns candidate experience and employer brand protection

BACKGROUND CHECK TEAM
• Conducts standard background verification (employment, education, criminal record)
• Triggers extensive background check only when required by role or initial findings
• Completes verification within defined SLA (48-72 hours)

HIRING MANAGER
• Defines position requirements and candidate profile
• Participates in screening interview
• Makes final hiring decision
• Extends job offer with HR support

TECHNICAL INTERVIEW PANEL
• Specialized interviewers assigned based on role requirements:
  - Coding/Algorithm Interviewer: For all technical roles
  - Frontend Specialist: For frontend/full-stack roles only
  - Backend Specialist: For backend/full-stack roles only
  - ML/Data Science Specialist: For ML/AI/Data roles only
• Conducts focused, non-overlapping technical assessment
• Provides clear pass/fail recommendation with scoring

BEHAVIORAL INTERVIEWER
• Assesses cultural fit, soft skills, and competency alignment
• Conducts structured behavioral interview (single session)
• Provides recommendation on candidate fit

TALENT ACQUISITION DIRECTOR
• Approves exceptions to standard process flow
• Reviews appeals on rejected applications
• Authorizes extensive background checks
• Approves offers exceeding defined salary bands

3. DETAILED STEP-BY-STEP PROCESS FLOW (IDEAL STATE)
================================================================================

3.1 START CONDITIONS AND TRIGGERS
----------------------------------

START EVENT: Candidate submits job application
• Entry Criteria:
  - Application submitted through official channels (career portal, recruiter referral)
  - All required fields completed (resume, contact information, work authorization)
  - Role-specific requirements acknowledged (location, salary expectations, start date)

Trigger: "Hand In Job Application" activity
System Actions:
• Assign unique application ID
• Log timestamp and source channel
• Route to HR recruitment queue
• Send automated acknowledgment to candidate (SLA: within 24 hours)

Volume Target: Process designed to handle current volume (8,000-9,000 applications/period)

3.2 PHASE 1: INITIAL SCREENING AND QUALIFICATION (Days 1-3)
------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1: Automated Resume Screening
Duration: <1 hour
Responsible: Applicant Tracking System (ATS) with HR oversight

Activities:
• Parse resume for required qualifications (education, years of experience, technical skills)
• Score application against position requirements using defined criteria
• Flag applications for HR review based on scoring thresholds
• Auto-reject clearly unqualified applications with respectful notification

Decision Gate 1A: Minimum Qualification Check
• PASS (Score ≥70/100): Route to HR telephonic screening queue
• FAIL (Score <70/100): Auto-reject with notification (Target: 25% rejection rate)

Outcome: Immediate rejection of 25% of applications (increased from 13.6% in AS-IS)
Rationale: More aggressive early filtering to prevent resource waste on unqualified candidates
Benefits:
• Reduces downstream interview load by 12%
• Improves candidate experience by providing faster feedback
• Allows HR team to focus on qualified candidates

STEP 2: HR Telephonic Screening
Duration: 15-30 minute call, scheduled within 48 hours of application
Responsible: HR Recruitment Team
Prerequisites: Application passed automated screening

Activities:
• Verify work authorization and availability
• Confirm salary expectations align with budget
• Assess communication skills and professionalism
• Validate resume accuracy (dates, titles, key responsibilities)
• Explain process overview and timeline expectations
• Answer candidate questions about role and organization

Decision Gate 1B: Telephonic Screening Pass/Fail
Evaluation Criteria:
• Work authorization confirmed: YES/NO (mandatory pass)
• Salary expectations within range: YES/NO (mandatory pass)
• Communication skills adequate: YES/NO (mandatory pass)
• Resume accuracy confirmed: YES/NO (mandatory pass)
• Professional demeanor demonstrated: YES/NO (mandatory pass)

Outcomes:
• PASS: Proceed to Background Check (Target: 70% pass rate)
• FAIL: Send rejection notification with feedback (Target: 30% rejection rate)

Target Metrics:
• 30% rejection after telephonic screening (increased from 11% in AS-IS)
• 48-hour maximum from application to telephonic screening
• 24-hour maximum from telephonic screening to decision notification

3.3 PHASE 2: BACKGROUND VERIFICATION (Days 3-6)
------------------------------------------------

STEP 3: Standard Background Check (Mandatory Gate)
Duration: 48-72 hours
Responsible: Background Check Team (internal or third-party vendor)
Prerequisites: Candidate passed telephonic screening

IMPORTANT SEQUENCING DECISION (IDEAL STATE):
Background Check is conducted BEFORE Screening Interview to:
• Eliminate disqualified candidates before consuming interviewer time
• Reduce late-stage rejections from verification failures
• Standardize process sequence (no more 50/50 split)
• Improve resource efficiency

Standard Background Check Components:
• Employment verification (last 2 employers or 5 years)
• Education verification (degrees claimed)
• Criminal record check (federal and state)
• Professional license verification (if applicable)
• Reference check (2-3 professional references)

Decision Gate 2A: Standard Background Check Clearance
Evaluation Criteria:
• Employment verified with no major discrepancies: YES/NO
• Education credentials confirmed: YES/NO
• No disqualifying criminal record: YES/NO (role-specific criteria)
• References provide positive feedback: YES/NO
• Professional licenses valid and current: YES/NO (if required)

Outcomes:
• PASS with no concerns: Proceed to Screening Interview (Target: 75% of cases)
• PASS with minor concerns: Flag for hiring manager review, proceed to Screening Interview (15% of cases)
• FAIL or MAJOR RED FLAGS: Proceed to Extensive Background Check (5% of cases)
• DISQUALIFIED: Send rejection notification (5% of cases)

STEP 3B: Extensive Background Check (Conditional Trigger)
Duration: 5-7 business days
Responsible: Background Check Team with Talent Acquisition Director approval
Prerequisites: Standard background check revealed concerns requiring deeper investigation

Extensive Background Check Components:
• Extended employment history verification (full career)
• Detailed criminal background check (county-level, federal databases)
• Financial background check (for roles with financial responsibility)
• Social media and online presence review
• Additional reference interviews
• Verification of gaps in employment history

Decision Gate 2B: Extensive Background Check Clearance
Outcomes:
• CLEARED: Proceed to Screening Interview with documented explanation
• DISQUALIFIED: Reject application with legal review (Target: 80% rejection rate for extensive checks)

Rationale for Upfront Background Check:
• AS-IS showed 1,391 + 1,389 = 2,780 rejections AFTER interviews due to background/screening
• Moving background check earlier eliminates 5% of candidates before interview investment
• Extensive background check in AS-IS had 100% rejection rate → should occur before interviews
• Reduces waste of interviewer time and candidate frustration

Target Metrics:
• 5% rejection after standard background check
• 4% routed to extensive background check (80% rejection = 3.2% additional rejections)
• Total 8.2% rejection during background phase
• 72-hour maximum for standard background check completion
• Background check completed BEFORE first interview 100% of the time

3.4 PHASE 3: SCREENING INTERVIEW (Days 6-9)
--------------------------------------------

STEP 4: Screening Interview (Hiring Manager + HR)
Duration: 45-60 minutes (scheduled, not walk-in)
Responsible: Hiring Manager (lead) and HR Representative
Prerequisites: Candidate passed background check
Scheduling SLA: Interview scheduled within 3-5 business days of background clearance

Interview Structure:
• Introduction and role overview (5 minutes)
• Hiring manager assessment of technical fit and experience (25 minutes)
  - Review of relevant project experience
  - Assessment of technical knowledge breadth
  - Evaluation of problem-solving approach
  - Discussion of career goals and motivation
• HR assessment of organizational fit (10 minutes)
  - Culture and values alignment
  - Team collaboration approach
  - Professional development interests
• Candidate questions (10 minutes)
• Next steps explanation (5 minutes)

Decision Gate 3: Screening Interview Pass/Fail
Evaluation Criteria (Structured Scorecard):
• Relevant experience demonstrated: Score 1-5
• Technical knowledge appropriate for level: Score 1-5
• Problem-solving capability: Score 1-5
• Communication and presentation skills: Score 1-5
• Culture and values alignment: Score 1-5
• Motivation and career fit: Score 1-5
• Overall recommendation: PASS (score ≥24/30) or FAIL (score <24/30)

Outcomes:
• STRONG PASS (Score 27-30): Proceed to Technical Interview Track (Priority scheduling)
• PASS (Score 24-26): Proceed to Technical Interview Track (Standard scheduling)
• FAIL (Score <24): Send rejection notification with feedback

Target Metrics:
• 40% rejection after screening interview (increased from AS-IS to strengthen gate)
• 60% proceed to technical interviews (reduced volume through better filtering)
• 24-hour maximum from interview completion to candidate notification
• 95% of interviews completed within scheduled time

Rationale:
• Strengthen screening interview as primary qualification gate
• Reduce technical interview volume by 40% through better early assessment
• Hiring manager involvement ensures technical relevance before specialized interviews

3.5 PHASE 4: TECHNICAL INTERVIEW TRACK (Days 10-15)
----------------------------------------------------

CRITICAL IMPROVEMENT: Eliminate circular interview patterns and standardize technical assessment

STEP 5A: Universal Technical Assessment - Coding Interview (All Technical Roles)
Duration: 60-90 minutes
Responsible: Coding/Algorithm Technical Interviewer
Prerequisites: Candidate passed screening interview
Scheduling SLA: Scheduled within 3-5 business days of screening interview

Interview Structure:
• Algorithmic problem-solving (40 minutes)
  - 2-3 coding problems of progressive difficulty
  - Assessment of data structures and algorithm knowledge
  - Code quality, optimization, and testing approach
• System design discussion (20 minutes)
  - High-level architecture thinking
  - Scalability and trade-off considerations
• Technical depth questions (20 minutes)
  - Computer science fundamentals
  - Role-specific technical concepts
• Candidate questions (10 minutes)

Decision Gate 4A: Coding Interview Pass/Fail
Evaluation Criteria (Structured Scorecard):
• Problem-solving approach and logic: Score 1-5
• Code quality and correctness: Score 1-5
• Algorithm and data structure knowledge: Score 1-5
• System design thinking: Score 1-5
• Technical communication: Score 1-5
• Overall technical competency: PASS (≥20/25) or FAIL (<20/25)

Outcomes:
• FAIL (Score <20/25): Send rejection notification (Target: 25% rejection rate)
• PASS (Score ≥20/25): Route to role-specific specialized interview

STEP 5B: Role-Specific Specialized Technical Interview (Conditional Based on Role)
Duration: 60-75 minutes
Responsible: Specialized Technical Interviewer (Frontend/Backend/ML - ONE ONLY)
Prerequisites: Candidate passed coding interview
Scheduling SLA: Scheduled within 2-3 business days of coding interview

CRITICAL SEQUENCING RULE: Candidate attends EXACTLY ONE specialized interview based on role:

Track A: Frontend Developer Role
• Frontend Interview ONLY
• Assessment focus:
  - JavaScript/TypeScript proficiency
  - Framework expertise (React/Angular/Vue)
  - HTML/CSS and responsive design
  - Browser APIs and web standards
  - Frontend performance optimization
• Decision: PASS → Behavioral Interview | FAIL → Reject

Track B: Backend Developer Role
• Backend Interview ONLY
• Assessment focus:
  - Server-side language proficiency (Java/Python/Node.js/etc.)
  - Database design and optimization (SQL/NoSQL)
  - API design and microservices architecture
  - Security and authentication
  - Backend scalability and performance
• Decision: PASS → Behavioral Interview | FAIL → Reject

Track C: Full-Stack Developer Role
• Frontend Interview AND Backend Interview (Sequential, NOT Circular)
• Mandatory sequence: Coding → Frontend → Backend → Behavioral
• Separate pass/fail decision at each stage
• NO LOOPS: Failure at either specialized interview results in rejection
• Decision: Both PASS → Behavioral Interview | Either FAIL → Reject

Track D: ML/Data Science Role
• ML Interview ONLY
• Assessment focus:
  - Machine learning algorithms and frameworks
  - Statistical analysis and experimentation
  - Data processing and feature engineering
  - Model evaluation and deployment
  - ML system design
• Decision: PASS → Behavioral Interview | FAIL → Reject

Track E: Full-Stack with ML Role (Rare)
• Mandatory sequence: Coding → ML → Frontend OR Backend (Not both)
• Maximum TWO specialized interviews
• Decision: Both PASS → Behavioral Interview | Either FAIL → Reject

ELIMINATED BEHAVIORS FROM AS-IS:
✗ NO Frontend ↔ Backend cycling (1,016 occurrences in AS-IS)
✗ NO ML ↔ Frontend cycling (1,791 occurrences in AS-IS)
✗ NO ML ↔ Backend cycling (273 occurrences in AS-IS)
✗ NO "try all interview types" approach
✗ NO zero-time-gap interview loops

IDEAL STATE RULES:
✓ Interview type determined by role requirements (documented in job description)
✓ Maximum 2 specialized technical interviews for any candidate
✓ Sequential progression only (Coding → Specialized 1 → [Specialized 2 if full-stack] → Behavioral)
✓ Clear pass/fail criteria with no ambiguity
✓ 3-5 business days between interviews (no zero-time transitions)
✓ Interview feedback documented within 24 hours
✓ Single technical interviewer per domain (no panel redundancy)

Decision Gate 4B: Specialized Technical Interview Pass/Fail
Outcomes:
• PASS: Proceed to Behavioral Interview (Target: 70% pass rate)
• FAIL: Send rejection notification with feedback (Target: 30% rejection rate)

Target Metrics for Technical Interview Phase:
• 25% rejection after coding interview
• 30% rejection after specialized interview(s)
• Total 47.5% rejection during technical phase (vs. late-stage rejections in AS-IS)
• Average 1.5 specialized interviews per candidate (vs. 3.8 in AS-IS)
• Zero circular interview patterns (vs. 3,080 loop occurrences in AS-IS)
• 5-7 business days total for technical interview phase (vs. weeks in AS-IS)

3.6 PHASE 5: BEHAVIORAL INTERVIEW (Days 16-18)
-----------------------------------------------

STEP 6: Behavioral Interview (Single Session)
Duration: 45-60 minutes
Responsible: Behavioral Interviewer (HR or designated behavioral specialist)
Prerequisites: Candidate passed all technical interviews
Scheduling SLA: Scheduled within 3 business days of final technical interview

CRITICAL IMPROVEMENT: Single behavioral interview session (no self-loop)

Interview Structure:
• Structured behavioral questions using STAR methodology (40 minutes)
  - Leadership and influence: 2 questions
  - Teamwork and collaboration: 2 questions
  - Conflict resolution and adaptability: 2 questions
  - Problem-solving and decision-making: 2 questions
  - Culture and values alignment: 2 questions
• Candidate questions and discussion (10 minutes)
• Compensation and logistics discussion (10 minutes)

Decision Gate 5: Behavioral Interview Pass/Fail
Evaluation Criteria (Structured Scorecard):
• Leadership capability: Score 1-5
• Teamwork and collaboration: Score 1-5
• Adaptability and resilience: Score 1-5
• Problem-solving mindset: Score 1-5
• Culture and values fit: Score 1-5
• Communication effectiveness: Score 1-5
• Overall behavioral fit: PASS (≥28/30) or FAIL (<28/30)

Outcomes:
• PASS (Score ≥28/30): Route to hiring manager for final decision
• FAIL (Score <28/30): Send rejection notification with feedback

ELIMINATED BEHAVIORS FROM AS-IS:
✗ NO Behavioral Interview → Behavioral Interview loop (1,032 occurrences eliminated)
✗ NO Application Rejected → Behavioral Interview flow (740 occurrences eliminated)

IDEAL STATE RULES:
✓ Exactly ONE behavioral interview per candidate
✓ No re-interviews or panels (behavioral assessment is comprehensive in single session)
✓ If panel input needed, concurrent observation rather than sequential interviews
✓ Clear documentation of assessment with specific examples
✓ Rejection decisions are final (no reversals without formal appeal)

Target Metrics:
• 15% rejection after behavioral interview (reduced from AS-IS through better upstream filtering)
• 85% proceed to offer decision (high pass rate indicates good upstream filtering)
• Zero behavioral interview loops
• 24-hour maximum from interview to decision notification

3.7 PHASE 6: FINAL DECISION AND JOB OFFER (Days 19-21)
-------------------------------------------------------

STEP 7: Hiring Decision and Offer Preparation
Duration: 24-48 hours
Responsible: Hiring Manager with HR and Talent Acquisition Director approval
Prerequisites: Candidate passed behavioral interview

Activities:
• Hiring manager reviews complete candidate file and all interview feedback
• Compensation package prepared based on role, level, experience, and market data
• Offer approval obtained (Talent Acquisition Director for standard offers, executive approval for exceptions)
• Offer letter prepared with all terms documented
• HR prepares offer presentation materials

Decision Gate 6: Final Hiring Decision
Evaluation: Holistic review of all interview feedback and qualifications
Outcomes:
• APPROVE OFFER: Proceed to offer extension
• REJECT: Send rejection notification (rare at this stage - <2%)

STEP 8: Job Offer Extension
Duration: Scheduled call or meeting, 30-45 minutes
Responsible: Hiring Manager with HR support
Prerequisites: Offer approved and prepared

Activities:
• Hiring manager extends verbal offer with enthusiasm and role context
• HR presents complete compensation package and benefits overview
• Discussion of start date, relocation (if applicable), and logistics
• Candidate questions addressed
• Written offer letter sent immediately following call
• Response deadline communicated (typically 5-7 business days)

CRITICAL IMPROVEMENT: Single offer extension (no offer loop)

ELIMINATED BEHAVIORS FROM AS-IS:
✗ NO Make Job Offer → Make Job Offer loop (861 occurrences eliminated)
✗ NO Application Rejected → Make Job Offer flow (690 occurrences eliminated)
✗ NO Make Job Offer → Application Rejected flow (110 occurrences eliminated)

IDEAL STATE RULES:
✓ Offer terms finalized BEFORE extension (no negotiations requiring new offers)
✓ Compensation band established with small negotiation flexibility built in
✓ Offer modifications handled through amendments, not new offer generation
✓ Rejections before offer stage only (no post-offer retractions except background check failure)
✓ Conditional language in offer for any pending verifications

Decision Gate 7: Candidate Offer Response
Candidate Decision Outcomes:
• ACCEPT: Proceed to onboarding (successful completion)
• DECLINE: Close application (unsuccessful, but graceful exit)
• COUNTER-OFFER: Single negotiation round within defined parameters
  - If within flexibility range: Approve amendment and re-extend
  - If outside range: Hiring manager and TAD determine approval
  - Maximum one counter-offer cycle

Target Metrics:
• 80% offer acceptance rate (vs. implied lower rate in AS-IS)
• Zero offer retractions (vs. 110 in AS-IS)
• Zero post-rejection offers (vs. 690 in AS-IS)
• 5-7 business day offer acceptance timeline
• 24-hour turnaround for counter-offer evaluation

3.8 END CONDITIONS AND OUTCOMES
--------------------------------

END EVENT 1: Successful Hire
• Candidate accepts offer
• Pre-employment requirements completed (final background check items, drug screen if applicable)
• Start date confirmed
• Onboarding process initiated
• Application closed with "Hired" status

END EVENT 2: Application Rejected (Multiple Possible Stages)
• Candidate fails at any decision gate with clear failure criteria
• Rejection notification sent with professional, respectful messaging
• Feedback provided where appropriate and legal
• Candidate marked as "Not Selected" with documented reason code
• Candidate may reapply after 6-12 months (per policy)

END EVENT 3: Candidate Withdrawal
• Candidate withdraws at any stage
• Application closed with "Candidate Withdrew" status
• Exit interview feedback collected where possible

END EVENT 4: Requisition Closed/Cancelled
• Position filled by another candidate
• Budget eliminated or role cancelled
• All active candidates notified
• Applications closed with "Requisition Closed" status

ELIMINATED END STATE ISSUES FROM AS-IS:
✗ NO rejection reversals (terminal state violations)
✗ NO self-loops on terminal activities
✗ NO ambiguous process completion

IDEAL STATE RULES:
✓ All end states are truly terminal (no outgoing flows)
✓ Every application reaches exactly ONE end state
✓ End state reason documented for reporting and analytics
✓ Candidate notification sent within 24 hours of end state

6.2 RISK MITIGATION STRATEGIES
-------------------------------

Risk 1: Resistance from Hiring Managers
Mitigation:
• Demonstrate time savings and quality improvements with data
• Involve hiring managers in process design
• Provide easy escalation for legitimate exceptions
• Track hiring manager satisfaction and address concerns

Risk 2: Candidate Push-Back on Process Length
Mitigation:
• Communicate clear timeline expectations upfront (3 weeks)
• Emphasize faster overall time vs. AS-IS (60% reduction)
• Provide excellent candidate experience throughout
• Benchmark favorably against competitors

Risk 3: Technical Interview Coverage Gaps
Mitigation:
• Build bench of trained technical interviewers across specialties
• Cross-train interviewers in multiple domains where possible
• Use structured interview guides to maintain consistency
• Calibration sessions to align interviewer standards

Risk 4: Background Check Delays
Mitigation:
• Negotiate aggressive SLAs with background check vendor (48-72 hours)
• Concurrent initiation where possible (after telephonic screening)
• Escalation process for delayed checks
• Backup vendors for capacity management

Risk 5: Process Rigidity Preventing Good Hires
Mitigation:
• Defined exception process with Talent Acquisition Director approval
• Ability to customize interview track for unique roles
• Fast-track process for critical or executive roles
• Regular review of decision criteria and thresholds

7. PROCESS FLOW SUMMARY (IDEAL STATE)

LINEAR PROGRESSION (No Loops or Reversals):

START → Hand In Job Application
  ↓
[Gate 1A] Automated Resume Screening → 25% REJECT
  ↓ 75% PASS
[Gate 1B] Telephonic Screening → 30% REJECT
  ↓ 70% PASS
[Gate 2A] Standard Background Check → 5% REJECT
  ↓ 90% PASS, 5% → Extensive Background Check
[Gate 2B] Extensive Background Check → 80% REJECT of subset (4% total)
  ↓ 91% PASS combined
[Gate 3] Screening Interview → 40% REJECT
  ↓ 60% PASS
[Gate 4A] Coding Interview → 25% REJECT
  ↓ 75% PASS
[Gate 4B] Specialized Technical Interview(s) → 30% REJECT
  ↓ 70% PASS (Role-based: Frontend OR Backend OR ML OR combinations for full-stack)
[Gate 5] Behavioral Interview → 15% REJECT
  ↓ 85% PASS
[Gate 6] Hiring Decision → 2% REJECT
  ↓ 98% PASS
[Gate 7] Job Offer Extension → 15% DECLINE
  ↓ 85% ACCEPT
END → Successful Hire / Onboarding

Alternative End States:
• Application Rejected (multiple exit points throughout)
• Candidate Withdrew (any stage)
• Requisition Closed (any stage)

Overall Conversion: ~25% from application to accepted offer

Key Characteristics:
✓ Strictly sequential flow (no loops)
✓ Clear decision gates with defined criteria
✓ Early rejection emphasis (46% rejected before interviews)
✓ Streamlined technical interviews (1-2 per candidate)
✓ Single behavioral interview
✓ Terminal states are truly terminal
✓ Fast and efficient progression (<200,000 time units)
