IDEAL Process Model

Process overview and purpose
- A streamlined, governance-compliant leasing lifecycle: application → appointment → viewing → paperwork → screening (with risk-based extensive screening when needed) → decision (reject or sign) → move-in → controlled rent collection cycles with clear late-payment escalation and orderly termination (tenant cancellation or eviction). The goal is to reduce waste and risk, shorten lead times, and prevent uncontrolled skips or rework.

Key stakeholders and roles involved (aligned with AS-IS inference)
- Prospective Tenant: applies, views, submits paperwork, signs, moves in, pays rent, may cancel.
- Property Management/Leasing Team: schedules, reviews paperwork, screens, decides, warns, accepts late payments per policy, evicts when necessary.

Detailed step-by-step process flow
Start conditions and triggers
- Trigger: “Apply for Viewing Appointment.”

Governed pre-leasing pipeline (application to decision)
1) Apply for Viewing Appointment
   - Gateway: Early eligibility decision using existing outcomes
     • If clearly ineligible or applicant withdraws interest, go to “Reject Prospective Tenant.”
     • Else proceed to “Set Appointment.”
   Rationale: Retains observed early rejections while making them explicit and criteria-driven.

2) Set Appointment → 3) View The Property
   - SLA: appointment offered within 24 hours; viewing within agreed window.

4) Post-viewing decision (single merge + decision)
   - If applicant not proceeding or fails basic criteria, terminate with “Reject Prospective Tenant.”
   - Else continue to documentation.

5) Hand In The Paperwork → 6) Check Paperwork → 7) Screen Prospective Tenant
   - Policy: screening only proceeds when paperwork is complete and validated in the “Check Paperwork” step (no bypass).

8) Risk-based screening decision (exclusive)
   - Low/acceptable risk → “Sign Contract.”
   - Uncertain/higher risk → “Extensive Screening,” then:
       • If approved → “Sign Contract.”
       • If not approved → “Reject Prospective Tenant.”
   Governance rule: remove all control-flow skips that would allow “Sign Contract” without “Check Paperwork” and “Screen Prospective Tenant.”

9) Sign Contract → 10) Move In
   - SLA: decision (Reject/Sign) communicated within 48–72 hours from completed paperwork receipt; move-in scheduled promptly post-signature.

Controlled post-leasing rent cycle
11) Monthly rent cycle gateway (deterministic policy)
   - On-time payment → “Pay Rent,” then loop to next cycle start date.
   - Missed payment → “Miss Rent Payment” → “Issue Warning.”
       • If paid within grace period → “Accept Late Payment” → return to “Pay Rent.”
       • If repeated delinquency or unpaid beyond policy thresholds → “Evict Tenant.”
   Policy clarifications:
   - Cap rework: only one accepted late payment per defined period; repeated/consecutive misses trigger eviction per policy (e.g., 2 consecutive cycles or X days past due). This eliminates indefinite oscillation between “Accept Late Payment” and “Miss Rent Payment.”

Orderly termination
- “Tenant Cancels Appartment” permitted only at cycle boundaries when there are no outstanding missed payments; otherwise resolve via “Accept Late Payment” or escalate to “Evict Tenant” prior to termination. This ensures financial closure before cancellation.

Decision points with criteria and outcomes
- Early eligibility (after Apply): Reject vs. Set Appointment based on minimum criteria (credit/income/availability); uses existing “Reject Prospective Tenant.”
- Post-viewing: Proceed vs. Reject based on applicant’s confirmation and basic checks.
- Screening: Exclusive choice among Reject, Extensive Screening, or Sign; Extensive Screening resolves to Reject or Sign only.
- Monthly cycle: Exclusive choice among Pay on time, Late but paid (via warning/acceptance), or Evict per thresholds.

Parallel activities and synchronization points
- The flow remains primarily sequential; no uncontrolled parallelism. Synchronization occurs at the decision points before “Sign Contract” and at the start of each rent cycle.

Exception handling and escalation
- Late payments: 1 grace-handling attempt (Issue Warning → Accept Late Payment). Beyond thresholds, escalate to eviction.
- Eviction: standardized criteria and documentation; terminal.
- Rejection: permitted only at defined gates (after Apply, after Screening/Extensive, or post-view if applicant declines), preventing ad-hoc midstream skips.

End conditions and outcomes
- Reject Prospective Tenant (terminal)
- Tenant Cancels Appartment (terminal, post-clearance)
- Evict Tenant (terminal)

Process metrics and performance targets
- Funnel performance targets
  • Conversion (Apply → Sign → Move In): raise from ≈ 27.7% to ≥ 35% by eliminating late-stage waste and clarifying criteria early.
  • Early rejection rate: concentrate rejections before viewing where possible (reduce View → Reject share from ≈ 1,710 to < 1,000 per 10,000 applies).
- Cycle-time SLAs (units aligned with current measurements)
  • Apply → Appointment offered ≤ 24 hours.
  • Viewing scheduled within ≤ 3 days of application where feasible.
  • Hand In → Check ≤ 24 hours; Check → Screen decision ≤ 24 hours.
  • Complete decision (Reject/Sign) within ≤ 72 hours after paperwork receipt; Sign → Move In within agreed timeframe.
- Post-move-in KPIs
  • Miss Rent Payment rate < 5% of cycles; Evictions < 1% of active tenants.
  • Late payment acceptance rate < 2% of cycles; zero indefinite late-payment loops.
  • Time from Miss Rent Payment to resolution (Accept Late Payment or Evict) ≤ defined SLA (e.g., ≤ 10 days).

Improvements made vs. AS-IS
- Eliminated control-flow skips that could allow signing without complete paperwork and screening; reinforced exclusive gateways at critical points.
- Shifted rejection earlier (post-Apply) to reduce wasted viewings/paperwork and shorten overall lead time.
- Instituted clear, finite late-payment handling (one grace acceptance, then escalate) to break rework loops and reduce delinquency.
- Guarded cancellation with a clearance check (no outstanding missed payments) to ensure financial closure before termination.
- Set explicit SLAs across the funnel to compress cycle times (appointment scheduling, paperwork checking, screening, decision).
